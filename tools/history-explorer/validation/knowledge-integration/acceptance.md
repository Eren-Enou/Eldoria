## Original-source adapter and boundaries

`knowledge.py` implements **experiment-021-trial/v1**,extractor4. All214 original
zero-based ordinals are indexed. Identity includes acquisition policy as well as
knowledge mode;Need/Acquire remain distinct. Archive member,compressed/decoded
hashes,record hash,source freeze,original IDs,timestamps and references are retained.
The original Trial is preserved. Reusing unchanged legacy physical record helpers
does not convert it to020's current-observation schema. Older018/019/020 retain
their adapters and ordinal conventions. Default CLI catalog has714 entries
(336019+164020+214021);018 remains optional.

Actor projections copy only the actual Choice.retained plus captured old local
input/public availability,candidates,priority and selection. A missing slot stays
unknown;no memory audit,attempt,final state or rival inventory fills it. The panel
shows cache,owner-local receipt,observation tick,age,last-seen stock,own debits and
remaining upper estimate. It explicitly distinguishes an available dated estimate
from unknown current stock. Expiry cause is shown from its original observer
transition,not invented as a reason inside a missing actor input.

Observer mode separately exposes actual stock,pending work,physical outcomes,
retention transitions and conserved material balances. Switching from an audit
moment to Actor shows only the exact own component captured there,with unavailable
cognitive fields labeled missing;the next actual decision supplies its own original
slot. Higher trust and more transfers are not universal benefits.

Typed observer links connect original observation→owner-local receipt→captured
retained state→actual choice→admission/busy interval→physical result→later context.
Receipt references use owner+receipt,not a globally omniscient acquisition identity.
Links do not reconstruct memory,make current stock known or imply sole psychological
causation. Later social/meal context retains attribution qualifications.

Comparisons distinguish storage,selection,admission,physical execution,period/common
tick material,terminal owned/cache material,legacy export,environmental slots and
full captured state. Semantic trust normalizes missing entries to0 and reports raw
map shape separately. FreshReference frequency and Unobserved channel confounds
remain explicit. The first execution field compares ordered recorded projections;
its left/right rows are not claims that one action counterfactually replaced the
other. A complete executed null may still have different environmental storage/audit.

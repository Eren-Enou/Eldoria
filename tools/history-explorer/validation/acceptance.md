## Limits and next research recommendations

Supported:complete valid018/019 trial archives and verified v1 viewer exports against
registered sources. Unsupported:001–017 schemas,arbitrary unregistered saves,separate
pre-epoch replay,fully clickable provenance graphs,live simulation control and
synthetic internal snapshots during atomic inquiry. Missing local assessment/progress
context is labeled rather than reconstructed. At observer events without exact point
links,latest captured tick state can precede the event or include other same-tick
phases;exact before/after references remain authoritative.

Observing the real histories makes repeated material stalls easy to locate. In
ongoing/Baseline core85,the first all-food-zero captured tick is258;130 later resource
events transfer zero food before end576. At101/106,actor0 still has food and H100
but invitation scores133/188 beat Consume100. These are archived heuristic choices,
not proof of irrationality or calibrated utility. The stock4 delayed-meal witness
shows how pulse timing can masquerade as effective cooperation.

Prospective trace improvements worth specifying:captured local Grouped assessment
and012 progress context at activity boundaries;explicit typed precursor/after-point
links for receipts/assessment transitions;clear preparation-versus-treatment markers;
and better separation of observed opportunities from artificial cohort/wave cadence.
Do not add missing past cognition retroactively. Motives,release forecasts,optimal
values and arbitrary-tick internal deliberation are not supplied by these archives.

Finite environmental acquisition remains a justified **candidate** for the next
behavioral study because current worlds can only consume/redistribute finite initial
food. It is not ready for implementation without a prospective design for local
resource observation,finite mass conservation,ownership/access,contention/arbitration,
real costs and grounded opportunity timing. Activity values and conservative admission
remain confounds. No production,economy,planner or Experiment020 is implemented here.

## Question and implemented architecture

Does actual selected activity and commitment change another person's legitimate
opportunities and persistent consequences beyond ordinary serial encounter execution?
Separate occupied pair, public observation, independent action, own material change,
later choice, executed social consequence, necessity of the extra activity, and
robustness. The first six do not imply the last two.

Five original modes are reconstructed: Serial is atomic resource execution with
History contact/Continue0 recipient and no voluntary eating; Committed steps that
same resource resolver; SerialActive adds voluntary own consumption to Serial;
Active adds it to Committed; NoHistory is Active with trust and episodes removed
only from the outgoing resource-ranking projection. Actual resource negotiation,
incoming valuation, inquiry, learning, trust and cognition still use their real
state. NoHistory is not amnesia. No016 contact-feedback cells are enabled.

Pure activity selection ranks Wait0, eligible Consume at own hunger, and the selected
old Invite at its old score. Strict greater-than preserves Wait,Consume,Invite ties.
Only the selected activity grants outgoing permission. Scores mix unlike heuristics;
they are not calibrated utility. All three individuals use the rule. There is no
privileged focal actor or resolver-selected correct outcome.

## Complexity, classification and protection suitability

Live state is one48-byte commitment payload plus environment/index/container overhead,
zero new cognitive cells. Per-resource-tick free-actor projection and observer capture
are real added costs. A233-step long world has701 points/1117 references, point payload
785,686 bytes instead of1,250,827 referenced repetitions, but full JSON8,695,059 bytes.
Primary+summary raw646,594,158/gzip9,741,175 bytes. Deduplication is useful locally;
observer histories remain expensive and unbounded. These bytes are not live RAM.

Original pure-policy medians858–890ns exclude projection/ECS; whole-world Serial/
Committed1.825/2.173ms and SerialActive/Active2.022/2.084ms reflect changed work counts.
Serialization17.668ms is separate. Legacy resolver32.320µs/world (two events) versus
committed24.455µs/step (one event plus policy/capture) cannot be subtracted for overhead.
No new benchmark is needed for this read-only review. More people/disjoint commitments
would require new consent/input/clock semantics; three-person results establish no scaling.

Demonstrated: actual occupation/current availability, independent consumption and a
persistent causal chain, plus a complete contact-only null. Mechanism-dependent:
heterogeneous scores, atomic queries, three people, Continue0, admission bounds,
period consumption, ID/order and cadence. Ambiguous: ecological prevalence, minimal
activity set, relationship versus food mediation and robust recurrence. Unsupported:
optimal welfare, fair matching, calibrated motivation, general autonomous ecology,
friendship, institutions, consciousness, intelligence or sustainable civilization.

No original prose requires numerical correction. Clarify that final-state null does
not mean identical internal observations, and more autonomy/eating does not imply
better cooperation/welfare. Protect the exact modes, lawfulness and narrow findings,
including all nulls/failures and these qualifications; do not protect broad autonomy.
Strongest open question: can a common elapsed-time need law create repeated consequential
activity conflicts beyond one finite initial-hunger alignment, without scripted renewal?

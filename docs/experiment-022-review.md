## Mandatory timing-registration qualification

The prospective architecture/gate describe absolute world-time hunger and sensing
boundaries. Actual State.epoch is comparison start; needs occur at `start+4k`, and
free decisions sense at `start+cadence*k`.64originals start off the absolute
four-tick phase. Original1 starts6 and first pulses10, whereas an absolute phase
would pulse8. The discrepancy is real, not harmless wording or demonstrated
equivalence. A historical implementation-notes paragraph still uses “absolute”;
its later disclosure and this review govern interpretation without rewriting it.

Each matched capacity pair starts at the same epoch and uses the same implemented
law. Tick-by-tick reconstruction confirms actual need, occupancy, completion and
conservation under that law. Thus the shared-clock and matched capacity findings
remain defensible. Absolute-phase invariance, exact adherence to that prospective
sentence and transfer across initial phases are not established. Additional
phase-sensitivity evidence is necessary for those broader claims, not necessary
to preserve the narrower demonstrated concurrency result. Investigate phase
explicitly in a separately preregistered scheduling study; do not amend the old
registration or replace its original outcomes.

The coarse registered release phase is also refined in implementation: core scenes
release their actors in their action pass, followed by scene/concern bookkeeping,
claims and meals. No intervening policy decision or incompatible same-actor work
occurs. Primitive ordering and actual release identity are verified; the prose
order alone is not empirical proof.

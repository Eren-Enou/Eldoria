# Research handoff after protected Experiments 001–013

2026-10-14. This is a research transition assessment, not an Experiment014
specification, mechanism commitment or implementation. The
[001–013 contract](experiment-contract-001-013.md) and accepted reviews govern the
foundation; all experimental policies remain optional where established.

## Established capabilities

- Resource interactions execute legally, conserve food and leave subjective episodes,
  learned relationships and persistent material history (001/002).
- Locally received testimony, fallible readings and legitimate attribution can revise
  beliefs/interpretations/credibility; hidden dependence can still mislead (002–008).
- Bounded unresolved concerns persist separately from episode memory and compete for
  inquiry; selected experiences update source/method expectations (005/006).
- Retention can change later inquiry, assimilation and executed consequences; no
  retention mode is a universal winner (009/010).
- Novelty differs from actual retained support/status change. StatusValue's qualified
  negative adoption result and012's progress/context findings preserve that distinction.
- Transient013 reconsideration can discover privately changed circumstances through
  a legitimate encounter and alter persistent execution. It can also waste effort,
  starve a useful Evidence method, or reinforce wrong information. Gate timing and
  general effort justification remain unresolved.
- Different lifetimes matter: trust/concerns/learning can survive cognitive eviction;
  an old episode can remain retained while excluded from the last-four policy window.
  No observer-history recovery is allowed.

These are conditional mechanism demonstrations, not general intelligence, calibrated
utility, psychological realism or naturally emerging social institutions.

## Laboratory support still supplied externally

Meetings, co-presence and resource scenes are scheduled by fixtures. Source inspections,
privacy/capability interventions and traffic pressure are controlled opportunities.
Voluntary selection/responses operate inside those opportunities, but their generation
is not autonomous. Longer histories preserve consequences without eliminating this support.

Inquiry effort advances recorded time without removing later fixture meetings or
pricing other activity. Hunger/circumstances and consumption are phase-controlled;
the world lacks independent resource production/acquisition, locally chosen movement,
encounter search and direct competition between cognitive and physical activity.
The finite initial food system measures transfers/consumption, not sustainable ecology.
These are missing experimental conditions, not instructions to implement all systems.

## Narrow plausible next question

Can existing locally learned inquiry/reconsideration affect the choice to spend a
genuinely scarce activity opportunity learning rather than taking an already available
material action, and can that choice help or harm later survival/resource state?

A small stationary environment with two or three persistent individuals, finite
initial food, competing concerns and a bounded public activity horizon could test
this. Make elapsed inquiry effort actually reduce the remaining opportunities for
existing resource interaction or consumption. Keep future partners, responses and
resource outcomes hidden; do not align every deadline with Pulse's phase. Preserve
missed material opportunities, wasted inquiries and misleading gains. A different
future may reverse an apparently sensible choice.

This would isolate cognitive versus physical opportunity cost before adding travel,
production or institutions. It should compare existing modes unchanged and test
whether adaptation remains meaningful when asking has consequences beyond an audit
tick. No formula, horizon, policy or014 fixture is frozen by this handoff.

## Architecture feasibility and risks

The existing ECS, stable identities, pure replaceable policies, explicit legality,
inquiry resolvers, time records, resource scenes and consumption provide a usable
base without a comprehensive rewrite. A separate opt-in experiment could add a
small actor-local activity projection and replaceable selector over existing actions.
This is a feasibility judgment, not an implemented cross-activity planner.

Current `inquiry_meeting` advances all participants and consumes response-dependent
time; scene follow-ups can automatically inquire. Neither interface currently enforces
an actor's remaining activity budget. An authorized design must address these paths
explicitly rather than silently changing legacy defaults or counting automatic
questions as free. Cost affordability must precede action; resolution must account
for actual effort without overspending, unrecorded refunds or hidden future replies.
Public time availability is not permission to expose the future encounter schedule.

Preserve concern age as local encounter experience, not a new elapsed-time oracle.
Keep learning selected/valid, provider-private state inside resolution, cognitive
caps unchanged, and inventory carried through consumption. Match initial schedules
and budgets across policies until voluntary divergence, then allow actual lost
opportunities and material state to persist. Capture skipped activities and causal
references; use exact replay plus independent accounting/locality checks.

Avoid movement/pathfinding, language, production economies, factions, institutions,
reputation networks or a general planner unless a later question demonstrably needs
them. History-copy overhead and growing audit storage are validation risks, not a
reason for an unrelated optimization pass. Establish a hypothesis and opportunity
accounting contract before changing behavior in any separately authorized experiment.

# Simulation Constitution

These ten requirements are established in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and apply
to every existing and future system. The [001–009 enforcement map](../experiment-contract-001-009.md)
records their mode-specific implementation and coverage.

1. **Identifiable causality.** Record the actual local input, selection, resolution
   and subsequent consequences. Explain old decisions from captured context, not
   by rerunning a newer policy.
2. **Information locality.** Policies receive only actor state and legitimate local
   observations/public context. Private partner components, observer indexes, hidden
   roots and future outcomes are not observation channels.
3. **Persistent consequences.** Preserve established learned effects and their
   separate lifetimes. Evicting an episode does not erase every relationship,
   expectation, concern or mode-specific evidence consequence.
4. **Individual perspective distinct from objective events.** Beliefs, interpretations
   and predictions may be wrong. Never consult truth to keep a listener correct.
5. **Explicit resource accounting.** Validate legality and participants before
   resolution. Record transfers, consumption, balances, costs and termination;
   interventions must not masquerade as naturally produced resources.
6. **Controlled reproducibility.** Pin rules/configuration/seeds and deterministic
   ordering. Keep wall-clock measurements outside deterministic evidence.
7. **No predetermined narrative outcomes.** Opportunities permit voluntary choices,
   including silence, failure and uncertainty; configuration does not force messages.
8. **Historical traceability.** Preserve original events/interpretations and append
   revisions and causal links. Serialize stable domain identities, never ECS handles.
9. **Replaceable behavior systems.** Keep policy projections and replaceable rules
   separate from legality, resolution, accounting and observer infrastructure.
10. **Measured complexity.** Bound cognition where established, audit eviction,
    document growing archives/maps and measure before optimizing or expanding scope.

An individual must never recover information merely because objective/observer
history still contains it. Separately established persistent state may legitimately
outlive the bounded representation that caused it. Retention is mode-specific:
preserve older derived consequences and Experiment 008's bounded local assessment
without imposing one cognitive model retroactively on another.

Derived indexes remain rebuildable accelerations of authoritative records; they
grant no new knowledge. Observer snapshots are audit exports, not resumable saves.
Controlled mechanisms and hand-selected heuristics do not establish consciousness,
psychological calibration, broad social emergence or unmeasured scalability.

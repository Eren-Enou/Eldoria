## Scientific question and registration

Can an actor-local change in ordinary consumption valuation change executed
physical/social history under continuous opportunities? This asks about an
implemented heuristic's causal consequences, not optimal survival behavior.

The fixed 56-world registration was published in
`80325c1e85bbdef0344d7b723618becbf0208ef8`, before runtime/evidence publication in
`d5fa6a721c7dce809ccc191e1ae86712dfac8463`; Git ancestry and the original
prospective/runtime records agree. Twenty-seven matched pairs and two reporting
placebos include inherited fixtures, seed sensitivities and a longer exhaustion
horizon. Seed repetition is parameter sensitivity, not independent statistical
population evidence. No post-outcome tuning is established or required.

## Exact implementation boundary

`prototypes/activity-valuation/src/model.rs` applies the pure selector after the
original legal candidates and pending-invitation masks. Baseline keeps original
scores. MealRelief adds `min(H,40)` only to an eligible Consume, giving
`H+min(H,40)`. Ineligible Consume remains −1. All other terms remain unchanged.
Selection uses a strict greater-than fold in Wait, Consume, Invite, Acquire order:
equal scores retain the first candidate. Integer min/add introduces no rounding.

The selector receives captured owned input and candidates, without scenario,
future, observer or partner-private state. It uses own current modeled hunger;
40 comes from the existing meal law. This is a heuristic on independently scaled
activity scores, not calibrated utility or predicted long-run relief. Eligibility
and incoming reply/admission paths are unchanged. Someone reserved by independent
consent may have no ordinary meal decision at that boundary.

The host generator makes explicit final-scoring substitutions in the frozen 023
coordinator, then reverses every substitution and asserts exact original text.
Physical reservation, response, admission, resource, claim, meal, sensing, need and
drain mechanisms remain inherited. Original runtime records establish 42 old byte
replays and 22 Continuous Baseline parity cases, with only the declared physical
schema difference. Fresh exact 024 replay confirms the published outcomes.

## Accepted interpretation and protection suitability

Demonstrated: local relative valuation can propagate into actual commitments,
completed actions, hunger, ownership and persistent directed relationships;
specific useful, adverse, null and materially reconvergent outcomes all survive.
The difference between start and completed effect is now explicit.

Mechanism-dependent: the current bonus changes comparison on inherited score
scales, suppresses tested extreme owned-food neglect and can crowd useful contact.
It does not guarantee eating: the original pure control retains Invite176 above
MealRelief Consume140. Empirical absence of treatment H100 nonselected meals in
the registered matrix is not a universal law.

Ambiguous: net value of social changes, mediation of every later transfer, and
generalization beyond engineered fixtures, fixed observation/phase/consent rules
and finite horizons. Unsupported: calibrated utility, optimal or human-like
behavior, general intelligence, renewable productivity, sustainable community,
universal superiority or default adoption.

Gate A passes for qualified scientific protection. Preserve original registration,
exact law and ties, isolation, source identity, benefits, harm0/1, exhaustion,
nulls, reconvergence and timing qualification. Protection must remain separate
from adoption and from the prospective design gate for delayed material work.
The strongest open question is whether locally known uncertain delayed yield can
compete with actual social opportunities without hidden future knowledge or forced
use of finished output.

## Accepted question and strength

Can an individual use personally experienced inquiry outcomes to learn context-sensitive
expectations influencing later voluntary decisions beyond existing006 novelty learning?

The additional assessment-change signal creates a genuine local learning distinction,
sometimes reaching executed persistent resource consequences. A small actor-observable
context boundary changes experience transfer across concerns. Preserve those as two
findings: the strongest executed progress result is shared by GlobalProgress and
Contextual, while the uniquely contextual core result demonstrates allocation/timing.
Neither proves universal superiority, default adoption, minimal or necessary new state,
source motive/competence understanding, truth-aware success or general metacognition.

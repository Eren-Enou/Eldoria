## Exact mechanism and information boundary

Unchanged is original015 History, including its recipient rule and zero contact
cells. Control preserves outgoing History but sets Continue to0. Recent shares that
response control and replaces its single record on every own outcome: failure lasts6
ticks, Met clears it. Typed retains at most2 partner/contact-type insertion-FIFO
records: Declined lasts12; AcceptedUnmet/Expired/Unavailable last2. Untyped retains
the same keys and capacity but every non-Met lasts12. Existing-key replacement keeps
its insertion position; Typed/Untyped Met removes only its matching key. Expiration
uses until<=now, both before choice and update; eviction never reads an archive.
Expiration is lazy: an all-Wait jump or consumption can leave an expired payload in
an observer's final vector until the next decision/update filters it. This does not
extend its eligibility or imply an autonomous reclamation event.

The pure chooser receives own old local input and own live records. Learning sees
only owner,partner,type,sent,received,outcome. Feedback arrives at the final wave tick,
after response/arbitration and any actual resolver. Incoming-only actors and Wait
receive no invented outgoing result. Declined reveals lack of consent, not another
person's score, motive, knowledge, food or future availability. AcceptedUnmet hides
the specific competing edge. Met means contact executed, not food or information
success. Resource Met does not clear an Inquiry delay. Expired/Unavailable exist in
projection/update tests but occur zero times in primary executed feedback. The
unavailable fixture excludes known-busy candidates; it is not evidence of learning
from unavailable invitations. No failed contact trains006/012 or fabricates resource
Refuse, trust, episode, evidence or concern resolution.

Old retained assessment, concerns, expectations, trust and episodes keep their own
lifetimes. Episode loss does not erase every established consequence. Neither new
chooser nor updater consults objective history to recover unavailable cognition.
The trusted resolver sees allocations to produce the narrow own signal; the actor
does not receive the wave. Privacy twins cover identical current actor inputs under
different hidden source willingness, not every possible future perturbation.

## Recipient control and ordering

Continue0 changes whether a recipient prefers incoming contact while leaving its
outgoing proposal permission intact. Incoming acceptance is provisional; arbitration
can execute that actor's outgoing edge instead. This is documented permission
semantics, not exclusive reservation or consent withdrawal. Actual execution still
requires the recipient's Accept. Control versus Unchanged changes arbitration,
encounters and food without learning, so those gains cannot be credited to feedback.
Control/Recent/Typed/Untyped share the same recipient rule and matched opening inputs;
their later incoming sets and choices are legitimate mediators after divergence.
Continue0 is a control, not a validated optimal response policy.

Ascending, Descending and Rotating affect proposer arbitration, not stable partner
or incoming tie order. Rotating depends on global wave ordinal; changed wave counts
change later phase. The second reader independently sums Control/Typed budget24
transfers as95/105,166/162,147/170. Consumption is109/107,104/104,114/111.
Matching is legal and reproducible, not neutral or fair. Reduced attempts do not
necessarily save shared time while another pair meets. Sender-active time is a
different quantity from elapsed world time.

## Limitations, corrections and classification

No material simulation defect was found. Initial unused import and equivalent
Clippy let-chain repair occurred before outcome inspection; source-freeze refresh
and supplementary extraction are disclosed in original prose. No outcome retuning
or historical rewrite is warranted. Clarify any reading of "learning" as calibrated
willingness: this is a finite retry rule; its signal does not explain motives.

Demonstrated: actor-owned finite feedback changes contact, actual missed inquiry,
real refusal/trust chains and sometimes persistent material distribution. Negative
Typed adoption evidence is defensible. Mechanism-dependent:6/12/2 delays, capacities,
replacement, resource ranking, provisional consent, Continue0, three-person serial
waves, conservative admission and stable order. Ambiguous: isolated mediation,
ecological prevalence, minimum cognition and welfare tradeoffs. Unsupported:
optimal allocation, generally useful cooldowns, calibrated willingness, human motives,
friendship, institutions, sustainable ecology or naturally emerging social environments.

Original measurements separate tiny live payloads (24-byte Cell, at most6 logical
cells plus allocation/resource overhead) from growing observer captures. Roughly1%
new wrapper overhead in one longitudinal serialization is not a resident-memory or
scalability result. Timing is separate from deterministic evidence; no benchmark is
needed for this documentation/read-only review.

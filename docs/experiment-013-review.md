# Experiment 013 scientific review

**ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION.** The implemented transient reconsideration rule
demonstrates earlier legitimate discovery and an executed persistent consequence
in a matched controlled case. Pulse also restrains Ungated's repeated probing and
can avoid its public-Evidence starvation. The evidence does not establish that the
four-age period is robust, that the added effort is worthwhile generally, or that
Pulse is preferable to existing learning. Preserve Pulse/Ungated as opt-in research
mechanisms; adopt neither as default. Scientific acceptance does not protect 013
as foundation: the protected contract remains 001–012. No 014 is started.

## 1. Question and competing explanations

Does 013 demonstrate that individuals can reconsider previously justified but
outdated expectations, discover changed circumstances through legitimate local
opportunities, and alter later behavior without privileged information or additional
persistent cognition?

| Separate question | Independently reviewed answer |
|---|---|
| A: Can existing learning adapt to changed opportunities? | Yes. Controls recover through lawful encounters, offers and changed local costs; they are not permanently fixed pessimists. |
| B: Can justified negative learning suppress discovery? | Yes. Low estimates and nonpositive scores delay discovery; absent encounters, known dependence and competing methods can prevent it entirely. |
| C: Does Pulse introduce actor-local reconsideration? | Yes. At the decisive local input it raises A Direct from −8 to 40, changes Pause to Ask, then receives new information. |
| D: Does its gate distinguish it from Ungated? | Yes in allocation and measured effort, including avoiding perpetual Direct crowding of Evidence. The successful private discovery does not require this gate. |
| E: Are the gains worth the additional effort over longer histories? | Unresolved. A narrow persistent chain coexists with substantially more unproductive queries and no general material improvement. |

The original A–E hypotheses in the [specification](../experiments/013/specification.md)
include a privileged-input/fixture explanation. Tested hidden/future pairs and the
runtime boundary exclude advance knowledge at those boundaries. Laboratory schedule
alignment remains a limitation, not evidence of a hidden oracle.

## 3. Exact finalized mechanism

`experiment013::evaluate` first invokes the unchanged 012 policy. Unchanged uses
the old inquiry decision; GlobalProgress uses context Any; Contextual, Pulse and
Ungated use the same Contextual learning. Eligibility is candidate-specific:

1. An own 006 concern/source/method cell supplies at least two attempts.
2. Its novelty expectation is at most integer `method.prior()/2` (Direct 27,
   Evidence 32, from priors 55/65).
3. Pulse additionally requires that candidate's active own concern age modulo four
   is zero. Ungated omits this condition.

Reconsideration computes
`gain = clamp(prior + credibility/10, 0, 100) * independence/100`, then
`benefit = max(current benefit, importance*gain/100)` and
`score = benefit - unchanged cost`. Division follows Rust integer truncation;
negative credibility division is not Python floor division. Known independence is
the existing 007 factor, zero or 100 in these fixtures. Zero cannot be bypassed.
Public offers retain their established attempted/seen handling and Evidence effort.
The prior is not an estimate that the provider has changed.

Existing concern/source/Direct-before-Evidence enumeration is preserved. Pause
begins at zero, and selection uses strict greater-than; the earlier candidate wins
positive ties, Pause wins zero ties. Eligibility need not raise benefit, raising
need not select a candidate, and selection need not acquire useful information.
The archived reconsidered count means the *selected* candidate's benefit was raised,
not every eligible candidate or every counterfactual change of winner.

Stored novelty/progress estimates are never reset. Selected valid experiences alone
train 006 novelty and the exact 012 binary support-or-status-change target. Silence
can train zero; Pause/invalid experience cannot train. Attempts, priors, half-prior
threshold and period four remain developer heuristics. No retuning occurred.

## 4. Information locality and causality

The runtime builds `q::Input` from own concerns/cells/seen signatures and public
co-presence/offers with own directed credibility. `p::Input` adds own retained
knowledge/hints. The self-evaluation adapter projects own live Grouped items and
own cells, never observer records. Pulse receives that same narrow decision/basis.
It reads no seed/scenario, objective truth, provider motives/competence/inventory,
future acquisition/reply/partner/outcome or observer history.

Provider knowledge/willingness enters resolution **after** selection. A legitimate
announcement exposes metadata, not its privately selected future payload. Native
self-knowledge and retained provenance replies preserve their established paths.
The typed Experience hook supplies own selected valid experience, own before/after
status, receipt presence, novelty/value and effort; support comes from own live items.
Observer record lengths and references audit chronology, not scoring or restoration.

Hidden-twin pairs preserve focal inputs and the first post-change decision despite
different privately informed sources. Mirrored futures preserve the pre-first-scene
inquiry prefix; later different encounters may legitimately change subsequent inputs.
Matching is required until the first voluntary divergence, not after consequences.
The existing independent reader also compares all participants' selected-query
prefixes, important because every participant uses the treatment mode.

## 6. Gate phase, local age and control recovery

Age is an established local-encounter counter. The three concerns enter round0 at
ages3/2/1 because earlier refusal scenes have different creation times. Inquiry
meetings advance active eligible concerns by one; resource encounters also advance
them. Inquiry effort does not advance age by elapsed ticks. Resolution stops the
active age progression, so divergence changes later phases.

For still-active core A before the first resource scene, Pulse's modulo-four
opportunities fall at rounds1/5/9/13 (ages4/8/12/16). At1 insufficient attempts
prevent renewal; at5 it renews A while the provider is still unproductive; at9 it
discovers the round8 acquisition. Staggered B/C ages and resource encounters mean
this is **not** a global one-query-per-four-meetings budget. Core later_useful's
raised selections are rounds5,9,10,14,15,17,18,21,22.
The gate supplies no exhaustion limit or stopping rule for an endlessly unproductive
active concern. Finite trial horizons bound the observed total; modulo-four renewal
can continue indefinitely if the established active local state and opportunities persist.

The first resource scene is after round15, deliberately exposing acquisition
timing. Coupled sensitivity preserves policies but shifts acquisition/quality/etc:

|---|---|---|

| Support40/quality80/change16/stakes2 | 16; 16; 16, support88 | First scene precedes acquisition; later transfers/material outcome match. |

Controls' round16 recovery is not spontaneous recognition of hidden improvement.
At round9 A Evidence score0 loses to Pause; after round15 consumption hunger falls
30→0, its cost31→28 and score0→3. The same lawful Evidence candidate now wins and
receives the retained source acquisition. Thus recovery combines old estimates,
an available encounter and changed local cost, not an information oracle.

The gate measurably restrains probing and protects some alternative opportunities,
so it is more than a pure fixed delay of Ungated. But period/phase is not isolated:
all acquisition rounds 8/12/16 and resource cadence16 share the period-four scaffold.

## 14. Demonstrated, dependent, ambiguous and unsupported

**Demonstrated:** same local inputs yield same pre-reply selection despite private
or future differences; selected failed experiences train; transient reconsideration
can accelerate legitimate discovery and affect executed persistent inventory;
controls can adapt; gated exploration spends less than Ungated and avoids a tested
Evidence starvation; misleading, unavailable, costly and missed cases survive;
longer histories preserve actual acquisition/eviction and legacy material causes.

**Mechanism-dependent:** exact integer scoring, stable ties, priors/half-prior/two
attempts, concern-specific modulo-four phase, Grouped claim suppression, importance,
cost/hunger, known-independence exclusion and binary change feedback explain those
results. No generic discovery theorem or source competence model is established.

**Ambiguous:** general usefulness of the gate, its schedule-phase robustness,
worthwhile exploration budget, sustained longer-history improvement, all-pair causal
mediation and whether an even simpler transient rule suffices. Added aggregate
change can be tiny or wrong, and material utility has no common scale.

**Unsupported:** optimal exploration, calibrated environmental volatility, general
competence/motive understanding, human curiosity/introspection, consciousness,
minimal learning architecture, population superiority or naturally emerging societies.

The provisional ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION is defensible for capability and research
preservation, with the two explicit prose corrections. Adoption remains unjustified.
The strongest remaining question is whether *selective* reconsideration reliably
beats the simpler ungated retry rule once phase alignment and endogenous opportunity
cost are independently controlled, while preserving useful Evidence opportunities
and legitimately learned failures. This review proposes no replacement formula.

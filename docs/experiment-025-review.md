# Experiment025 scientific review

## Decision: ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION for the narrow research finding

The evidence demonstrates voluntary finite material preparation, genuine later
food use, real social transfers and persistent material/social consequences in
these controlled four-person worlds. It does not establish sustainable production,
good allocation, optimal work, a productive community or a justified global default.
No025protection or026follows from this review.

The question is whether adding a locally known, finite preparation affordance under
unchanged continuous opportunities can produce voluntary work and meaningful later
consequences, without scripted work/food use or hidden knowledge of stock/futures.
The result is positive within this specific affordance, heuristic and fixture set.

## Mechanism-dependent

Prepare's `H − known duration` score, food0eligibility, old-first tie order, raw/ready
single slot, stable allocation order, cost law and existing social/inquiry heuristics
shape these results. They are explicit heuristics, not calibrated utility, learned
productivity or psychological laws. Different current work costs change timing;
the experiment does not select an optimal threshold. The finite conversion law
defines symbolic food/waste separation and conserved units, not energetic creation.

Material persistence is partly built into the law: waste remains after food is
eaten, so an access-closed arm cannot fully reconverge with an arm that prepared.
Trust/hunger divergence and independently chosen later use are stronger behavioral
evidence than waste alone. Food holdings and full material are reported separately.

## Negative, ambiguous and missing evidence

Primary records contain117attempts,77successes and40failures. Failure is not deleted
as noise. Five successful outputs are unused at the horizon, but none has a later
ordinary meal option. **Persistent voluntary neglect is not demonstrated.** Choosing
Invite over an eligible Consume is different from lacking another decision slot.

Blocked invitations demonstrate an actual missed protocol opportunity, not that a
useful exchange would have happened. Inquiry occurs in only records19–21; these
fixtures do not establish general effects of production on inquiry or learning.
The apparently harmful sharing trajectory does not establish a transfer-specific
counterfactual or a universal welfare metric.

Absolute phase at bootstrap0and the rotating final-batch sibling are inert in the
realized setup; seed variants are sensitivity checks, not independent samples.
RawOnly omits ready-cache observation in both arms. The closed arm is a fair access
control but is not old-world feature-off parity. Finite pile sizes, four actors,
fixed profiles, one site and controlled observations limit external relevance.

## Locality, persistence and complexity

Prepare receives only own hunger/food/availability, own retained source information
and a public time bound. It receives no actual rival stock, private partner state,
future opportunity, scenario label or observer history. Actual extraction checks
world stock only in resolution. Observer-only global scene translation never
returns a value to live simulation or policy input. The independent reader checks
the actual exported local coordinates, not the validation copy.

One tagged environmental slot remains bounded; losing that representation does
not erase already prepared food, escrow/waste, trust, concerns or other legitimate
persistent state. Those effects cannot recreate forgotten source knowledge.
Original005–009retention distinctions and001–024defaults remain intact.

Additional live state is one finite raw source, at most four active work records
and the already constrained per-actor source slot. Observer history is growing:
168.3MBdecoded/4.47MBcompressed for50records. The temporary final validation clone
also has cost. Median2.64s warm exact replay includes validation/I/O and is not a
pure simulation benchmark. Sampled process working set is not bounded cognition.
No general optimization or audit-infrastructure refactor was performed.

## Unsupported and future integrity boundaries

Do not protect claims of optimal work, human-like labor, general planning, broad
social emergence, biological survival, renewable resources, institutions or a
self-sustaining community. Do not count more transfers as uniformly better.
No global mechanism adoption is recommended by this narrow result.

A later integrity pass could protect the fixed conversion/effort/locality contract,
the actual preparation→later-use existence evidence, full-cost stale failures,
costly distribution, nulls, reconvergence distinctions, missing voluntary-neglect
witness, and transparent observer corrections. This review does not extend the
001–024foundation itself.

The strongest remaining question is whether bounded actor-local evaluation can
learn when to prepare, consume and share without treating output or social transfer
as inherently valuable regardless of its later individual consequences. This
experiment establishes the causal substrate for that question, not its answer.

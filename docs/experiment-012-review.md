## Finalized mechanism and local inputs

`src/self_evaluation.rs`, its simulation adapter, and the guarded provenance hooks
implement the documented formula without retuning:

- Unchanged captures/updates new cells but returns the exact old selection. It
  ablates behavioral use, not storage or capture cost. GlobalProgress uses Any;
  Contextual partitions the same source/method experience by two observable contexts.
- Context comes from the actor's live retained Grouped items for its active concern:
  any positive-quality non-Claim item means EvidencePresent, otherwise ClaimFallback.
  It is the assessment fallback boundary, not a learned expertise or motive category.
- A live cell contains source, method, context, saturating attempt count, expected
  change and last inquiry reference. Cap32 per actor; insertion FIFO. Updating an
  existing cell changes it in place without refreshing insertion order. This differs
  from old006 cells' remove-and-append lifetime and is explicit, not an inherited
  universal eviction model.
- Priors are Direct55/Evidence65. A selected valid learning outcome gives target100
  if signed support or selected concern status changes, otherwise0. Update is
  `(old + target)/2`, integer truncation. Silence can train0; invalid replies and
  Pause do not train. Attempts are not independent samples or confidence weights.
- With a matching cell, effective gain is
  `min(existing gain, max(expected change, existing opportunity value))`.
  Benefit becomes `importance * effective gain /100`, minus unchanged cost.
  Missing cells preserve baseline scores. Candidate `expected_gain` and provenance
  factors still describe the old signal;012 changes benefit/score/selection. The
  independent reconstruction derives the effective gain from the referenced cell.
- Public untried offers can limit discounting; old seen/attempted-offer rules still
  apply. The minimum preserves007's zero-independence exclusion. No new future-aware
  opportunity value enters. Pause starts0; strict greater-than preserves stable
  concern/source/Direct-before-Evidence enumeration, including nonpositive ties.
- Enablement is idle-only, after Grouped, prospective with empty cells and a first
  inquiry boundary, fixed-mode and mutually exclusive with CurrentNeed/StatusValue
  in both enablement orders. Policies remain replaceable. Defaults stay unchanged.

The binary target counts one-point change, conflict, resolution and mistaken change
alike. It is developer-chosen feedback semantics learned from actor experience,
not a calibrated probability, truth accuracy, evidence magnitude or utility estimate.
Only `expected_change`, not attempts or the reference itself, affects this discount.

Selection receives own bounded006/007 state, at most eight own active bases computed
from at most32 live items, own new cells and public co-presence/offers. Inspecting the
runtime call path found no objective truth, hidden competence/provenance, scenario
label, seed, future partner/relevance, pre-selected actual answer or partner-private
component in the new scorer. Provider knowledge and willingness enter resolution
only after selection; they do not choose the inquiry target.

The typed `Experience` update receives actor identity/selected action, valid learning
flag, own before/after status, current receipt presence, novelty/value and elapsed
effort. It does not receive the full response record, responder-private input or
observer history. Support is computed from own live items before/after. Global
assessment record lengths in Pending are audit boundaries, not cognition or scoring
inputs. Observer records are appended, never searched to restore live cells/content.
Last-inquiry references are trace handles, not a recovery channel.

## Lifetimes, added state and design limits

Evidence eviction changes live assessment/context, not every established causal
consequence. The new estimate has its own bounded lifetime and stores no event,
claim, quality or support content. Eviction of a cell loses that estimate; fresh
learning starts from the prior rather than the audit. Old belief/signature/concern,
trust and novelty-cell lifetimes remain distinct and unchanged. Forgetting here
means unavailable008 basis, not simultaneous deletion of all earlier representations.

Across180 full archived trials, no new learning-cell eviction occurs and maximum
observed occupancy for any actor is7. The32-cell capacity/FIFO loss behavior is tested
synthetically by an existing regression; it is not an executed ecological capacity
result. Assessment/provider-knowledge pressure is real in the named forgetting cases.

The extra cells summarize historical change across concerns; old006 cells summarize
novelty per concern. Common source/method IDs and attempt metadata overlap, but feedback
and transfer keys differ. Actual matched decisions exclude old006 alone as the explanation
for all012 behavior. They do not exclude a transient context-aware scorer or reflection
using still-retained episodes as simpler alternatives. Neither was evaluated. The first
progress divergence is within A, so it does not by itself justify persistent cross-concern
state. The context ablation isolates transfer, but not necessity versus every simpler design.
No evidence proves32 cells minimal/optimal or supplies an adoption cost-benefit threshold.

15 hand-designed families share a three-concern refusal scaffold, fixed intervention
profiles and sources, and deterministic accurate/inverted readings. Some families overlap.
All mode-specific external schedules match; only voluntary responses/time and resulting
worlds can diverge. The policies do not receive remaining slot budgets or future partner.
Training is actual accounted behavior, not free preparation. The three sensitivity
configurations couple support, quality, importance, effort, ordering, slots, sources,
partner and stakes. They are neither isolated-factor estimates nor external transfer.

## Complexity, classifications and later protection

Archived fixed-input measurements use19 candidates, five repeats of50,000 evaluations
and equal decision cloning:32 supplied cells give medians761ns Unchanged,865ns Global,
958ns Contextual. This excludes projection/update/ECS dispatch. All live maps serialize
to209–795 bytes; new observer records3,498–7,513 bytes/trial. JSON bytes are not RAM.
Whole-trial timing mixes diverging behavior, export and validation; it does not isolate
CPU overhead. The baseline's observer-only capture also incurs new storage. No new
runtime benchmark or optimization is needed for this documentation/offline review.

**Demonstrated:** local experienced updates separate from novelty; context-dependent
transfer/allocation; legitimate and misleading acquisitions; executed persistent progress
chains and negative reversals; real forgetting/redelivery; exact scores/ties/accounting
and archive equality. A claim that012 is merely observer explanation or all choices
equal006 is falsified within these fixtures.

**Mechanism-dependent:** binary change target, two fixed contexts, inherited55/65 priors,
moving average, conservative discount, public offer handling, claim fallback and stable
ties. An incorrect change can teach optimism; no change can teach useful opportunity
expectations. Feedback measures neither calibrated usefulness nor truth.

**Ambiguous:** persistent state's necessity versus transient/episodic alternatives,
long-run exploration under obsolete experience, independent held-out factors, utility
of donor/recipient outcomes, and whether costs merit adoption in naturally arising
encounters. There is no demonstrated contextual executed advantage over Unchanged
in sensitivity; the core progress advantage is shared with GlobalProgress.

**Unsupported:** consciousness, human introspection, broad metacognition/general
intelligence, motive understanding, truth-aware or optimal learning, natural social
emergence/prevalence, statistical significance or population scalability.

A later integrity pass should protect these narrow findings together with all
negative outcomes, the exact opt-in formula/cap/ties/lifetimes, the distinction between
progress and context effects, repair disclosure, corrected denominator interpretation,
and no unique contextual material superiority/minimal-state claim. This review creates
no001–012 contract. Strongest remaining question: can a bounded individual revisit an
obsolete poor expectation without a competence oracle or unlimited exploration, and
does a separate persistent progress map help more than simpler retained-state inference?

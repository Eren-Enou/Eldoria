## Research handoff and remaining limitations

The evidence supports narrow experiential distinctions, not preferable/minimal architecture.
Ordinary fixtures reach at most7 cells and no learning-cell eviction;32-cell loss is
synthetic coverage. Transient context-aware scoring and retained-episode reflection remain
untested alternatives. Short designed histories, deterministic readings, overlapping families
and coupled sensitivity factors do not establish independent effects, natural prevalence,
psychological calibration, consciousness, broad emergence or population scalability.

Future research questions, not results or an013 plan: how to revisit learned poor
expectations when circumstances change; how to keep exploration possible under limited
inquiry; when persistent experience summaries justify their cognitive cost; whether a
simpler transient/episodic mechanism reproduces these effects; and what persists in
longer less externally controlled histories. This pass changes no mechanism and starts
no013. All previous protections are inherited intact.

## Question and authority

Do choices among eating, resource contact, inquiry and waiting remain stable under
reasonable priority and opportunity-timing changes with shared recurring physics?
The archived specification, original compressed records and actual implementation
are authoritative. Reproducing an evaluator label alone would not answer this.
The review inspected the six selectors, idle adapter, fixture, recurring stepper,
invitation/response/arbitration code and original independent readers. The
[original results](../experiments/019/results.md) remain unchanged.

## Independent verification and scoring

The read-only `validate019` replay checks 1,296 worlds and 336 raw complete histories,
including byte comparisons, source/archive manifests, locally reconstructed
cognition, physics, admission, consent, resource behavior and 13 corruption classes.
Its primary reader shares reviewed earlier cognitive primitives; it is not wholly
independent of all previous validation. Its complementary prefix reader imports
neither the scorer nor evaluator and checks 840 paired prefixes, 317 complete final
nulls and 162 timing/order initial equalities. The existing 21 registered causal
slices were inspected against the primitive source paths and executed records.

A new complementary [scope reader](../examples/support/review019_scope.py) imports
none of those readers or the evaluator. It directly checks 142,248 recorded activity
selections, strict first-maximum ties, EatFirst/Deadline priorities, 46,524 provisional
responses, 152 actual queries and per-trial food conservation. It independently
recomputes all six 216-pair all-seed summary contrasts, agrees with every original
count, finds the same 317 complete final nulls and 343 material-reconvergent pairs.
The latter counts overlap, include the focused histories and are not independent
samples or 343 distinct experimental replications.

Baseline/EatFirst/NoHistory are exact historical controls. Scale90/110 scale only
the eligible top Invite score with integer truncation; they leave within-contact
selection unchanged. Deadline differs from EatFirst only where eating first would
consume the selected contact's last admissible public-bound slot. Strict order is
Wait, Consume, Invite. Quantization at small scores is not a smooth sensitivity.
NoHistory masks outgoing resource ranking, not stored cognition or negotiation.

## Causal witnesses challenged

* **Useful contact:** original `seed-42.jsonl.gz`, records 103/107, seed 42,
  default configuration, Baseline/Scale110. Identical actor-0 input has food 2,
  hunger 80; scores [0,80,78] versus [0,80,85]. Baseline consumes at 1 and executes
  source-1 Offer at 3 / actor-0 Accept at 4. Scale110 executes actor-0 Request at 3,
  source-1 Offer at 4 / actor-0 Accept at 5. Transfer direction, duration and later
  history really differ. Area [10960,4505,10200] versus [8355,6685,10200] is a tradeoff:
  actor 0 improves while actor 1 worsens; final hunger [100,95,100] versus all 100
  precludes a universal benefit claim.
* **Private reversal:** records 109/113, unproductive_contact. The same initial
  focal input and scaled invitation meet privately different partner circumstances;
  the partner eats and declines. No transfer occurs. Actor-0 area worsens from
  10640 to 11680. This varies generosity and caution together; it does not isolate
  a single private trait. Seed-1 useful-contact also reverses the actor-0 gain,
  with seeded expectations and subsequent feedback not separately isolated.
* **Deadline:** held archive records 182/183, ongoing stock 4, EatFirst/Deadline.
  Prefix matches to tick 16. The heuristic selects outgoing 0→1, but the actual
  commitment is 2→0. Source 2 Leaves at 19 (event 6). Actor 0 consumes at 17 versus
  20, removing hunger 5 versus 10. Its advantage persists through ticks 24/48/72
  and eventually reconverges. This is delayed-meal efficiency after wasted contact,
  not evidence of better cooperation. Window-48 results contain opposing costs.
* **Information:** held records 7/10, Descending Baseline/Scale90. At tick 45,
  period 0 step 3, planned inquiry score 38 becomes 34 below Consume 35. The
  Baseline inquiry is NOT executed there: matching selects a resource encounter.
  Actual later queries at 51→63 and 51→64 differ: no receipt/no change versus
  concern 0 support 20→60/Resolved. Later feedback is a mediator, not proof that
  all material effects originate solely from improved knowledge.
* **Conservative admission:** default useful-contact Baseline record 103 versus
  short-window held record 49. Same initial state/profiles; actual Offer 3/Accept 4
  fits four ticks, while the public eight-tick bound excludes resource contact in
  the six-tick window. This is a retrospective feasibility witness, not information
  an actor had or proof every excluded encounter would succeed.
* **Nulls and reconvergence:** 317 complete final-Snapshot null pairs are verified,
  rather than inferred from equal food/hunger. EatFirst/Deadline has eight primary
  transient material differences, seven crossing multiple period boundaries, all
  materially reconvergent. Material reconvergence does not erase executed history.

## Accepted, negative and mechanism-dependent findings

Scale90 changes terminal material in 0/216 configurations, Scale110 in 2/216, yet
some contacts, queries, trust and intermediate state change. Small perturbations
are usually terminally inert in these fixtures, not universally harmless. Deadline
changes behavior versus EatFirst but never terminal material or total consumption
in those 216 comparisons. Its primary areas are equal 44/54, lower only 2, higher
only 2 and mixed 6. No general superiority follows. All reported all-seed pair
consumption quantities match. More transfers do not mean more food production.

History helps and harms. Mutual Baseline/NoHistory transfer 8/4 times and actor-0
area is 8280/9925 with equal terminal material; history-family outcomes contain
opposing individual effects. Useful, wrong (20→−30/Partial), redundant (10→10)
and empty information remain distinct. A response is not necessarily productive.

Windows, gaps and order can change real histories. The axes cover different
families, so their raw counts do not rank general effect size. Gap changes alter
meal/pulse alignment, concern age and Rotating wave phase together. Windows can
reopen immediately; exclusion may delay rather than permanently prevent contact.
Long 576-tick trials exhaust finite food and often saturate hunger while contacts
continue. Endpoint equality can obscure sustained earlier differences.

## Locality, controls and limitations

Captured decision inputs contain own Agent/profile, now/end, public availability
and own query projection. Private donor stock/profile, actual future duration,
scenario labels, observer history and hidden provenance do not enter the selectors.
The original reader checks local cognitive prefixes and private twins. It does not
restore evicted cognition; separately persistent trust/concerns retain their old
lifetimes. Recipient consent, arbitration and actual execution remain separate.
Physical pulses and meals use unchanged 018 semantics and actual shared time.

The causal evidence is strong for particular executed histories, not ecological
frequency or policy optimality. Three actors, one resource commitment, atomic
inquiry, Continue0, synchronized pulse phase, finite stock, external windows and
incomparable heuristic score units constrain every conclusion. Fixed deterministic
seeds are reproducibility/sensitivity controls, not independent statistical evidence.
The information case has continuing-treatment and feedback confounds. Neither
human psychology, expected utility, fairness nor sustainable society is established.

## Corrections and gate

No numerical correction or material implementation defect was found. Preserve the
original qualification that `activity_trace_changed` omits target/type: use the
complete voluntary-choice prefix comparison. Do not describe an outgoing edge as
the actual matched edge, a preview as a query, lower hunger area as global welfare,
or equal terminal material as full-state equality. Source-freeze manifests identify
the historical implementation; module declarations and observer tooling need not
keep every aggregate shared source file byte-identical forever.

Gate A: PASS. Independent review
is separate from original outcomes and from policy adoption. The next scientific
question remains whether these effects survive externally grounded opportunities
and independently justified activity valuations; do not retune this completed study.

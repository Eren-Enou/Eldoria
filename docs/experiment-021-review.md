## Exact question and independent method

Can legitimately received, bounded, dated environmental information change later
voluntary activities and material/social history when other individuals change
stock without updating the observer's knowledge? This is intermittent passive
sensing at one stationary, monotonically depleting cache, not active exploration.

The original specification, gate, registered matrix, source locks, complete original
histories, comparisons, results, costs, tests and three implementation modules were
examined. Preregistration commit `ee1eb6b` precedes treatment/outcome commit
`20b6871`. The disclosed post-registration adapter formatting is whitespace only;
the later semantic-trust reader changes interpretation, not outcomes.

The new [complementary reader](../examples/support/review021_scope.py) uses only
the Python standard library. It imports no evaluator, earlier reader, prepared
analysis or simulation scorer. From original histories it reconstructs all physical
operations and captured food/cache balances, hunger progression/area, owned
environmental transitions, acquisition eligibility, outer activity scores, strict
first-maximum ties and descriptive matched contrasts. It checks **68,027** available
actor selections, including **114** acquisitions with an overestimated stock.
At each first changed selection the old local input is identical across modes.

This is complementary independence, not an independent implementation of every
historical cognitive theory. Legacy contact-candidate scores, inquiry assessment,
concern transitions, consent and detailed time bookkeeping are separately checked
by the unchanged original primitive reader and exact Rust replay. The new reader
reconstructs outer allocation from recorded legacy contact candidates and does not
pretend to independently rederive their inner inquiry scores. Neither reader uses
the History Explorer or presentation caches as authority.

## Locality, bounded retention and physical law

The new Input contains only own legacy input, optional own Cell, public duration
and inquiry-lane availability. No mode, scenario, future sensing schedule, actual
response, rival inventory, hidden provenance or observer record enters selection.
Own legacy components and public availability are captured at the decision point.
Private-helpful/cautious controls and negative tests preserve the locality boundary.

Each actor owns one slot, an owner-local receipt counter, immutable dated observation
and own-take count. The estimate `max(0, observed_stock-own_takes)` is only an upper
bound under this depletion-only physics. Rival success cannot update it. Own success
updates a still-available slot without rejuvenating its date. New Sense/Depleted
experiences receive new local receipts. Unknown remains None, distinct from known 0.

Expire12 removes age≥12 before use/result update and at final export. Success after
expiry does not copy quantity from the pending attempt or observer archive.
Commitment target identity and acquired food may persist legitimately. CurrentOnly
uses a fresh current receipt then discards; Unobserved has no channel. Historical
audit retains observations, before/after slots, attempts and outcomes for validation
only. It is not a policy recovery mechanism. Legacy trust, concerns and learned
effects retain their established separate lifetimes.

The independent physical reconstruction preserves owned+cache+consumed after every
operation and every captured point. Transfers debit/credit owners; claims revalidate
actual stock at their deterministic deadline/rank; failed claims create nothing;
meals remove one unit. Hunger advances once per global elapsed interval (+5/4 ticks,
cap 100) before same-tick effects. Actor reservation excludes incompatible work.
Original full replay/reader separately checks consent, actual deadlines and old
resource/cognitive semantics. Atomic inquiry remains conservative and exclusive.

## Passive sensing and interpretation limits

CurrentOnly/LastSeen/Expire12 have the same exogenous glimpse slots and matched
physical/cognitive starts. Their first changed choice has identical old local input;
retained availability changes eligibility. Later busy states can cause missed
glimpses as consequences, not a mode-specific external schedule. This establishes
a causal retention effect **under that intermittent channel**, not universal value.

FreshReference receives free current measurements at every free decision. In
ongoing **80**, all three hunger areas `[42915,35110,43525]` are lower than LastSeen
82, with 38 rather than 43 transfers. It is a strong simple alternative. More free
sensing neither isolates retention efficiency nor proves memory unnecessary when
sensing costs time or cannot occur. Unobserved is channel ablation, not ordinary
forgetting. Neither control justifies broad intelligence comparisons.

No mode values age/risk or chooses whether to observe. Need/Acquire, strict ties,
passive cadence, single-slot overwrite and age12 expiry are experiment-specific
heuristics. Fixtures are selected diagnostic cases with prepared history, three
stationary people, one cache, fixed windows and capped needs. No production,
negative-to-positive environmental correction, travel, multiple sources, survival,
general memory optimality, psychological realism, naturally arising community or
Eldoria canon is established.

## Architecture, cost and protection suitability

One slot bounds live environmental content; receipt counters contain no episodes.
Observer histories grow. Original trials occupy **751,189,037 raw bytes** and
**14,880,167 gzip bytes**; full trace serialization and duplicated legacy snapshots
dominate storage. The original engineering run reports matched FreshReference
execution/capture +7.7% and raw size +4.5% versus 020; these are one ordered run,
not calibrated general costs. Other-mode timings mix changed histories. Peak RSS
was not measured; the offline archiver materializes the entire raw trial file.

The isolated 614-line lifecycle adapter preserves frozen 020 but duplicates clock,
reservation, completion and need-synchronization logic. It still assumes three
actors/one social scene and atomic inquiry. Exact fresh controls constrain drift;
they do not prove a multi-scene scheduler. Refactoring protected adapters solely for
reuse would create unnecessary compatibility risk. Concurrent shared-world work
requires its own prospective ownership/time contract.

Protect the demonstrated receipt ownership, dated retained state, bounded slot,
expiry/no-recovery, physical revalidation, actual useful/stale chains, mixed
distribution, nulls, material reconvergence/social persistence and controls.
Do not protect a claim of general advantage, active sensing, sustained supply or
community capability. No material implementation defect was identified in review.

Strongest open scientific question: when observing is a voluntary, costly activity,
does retaining environmental information justify its opportunity costs? The separately
authorized 022 scheduling study addresses a physical prerequisite, not that valuation
question, and must not be presented as answering it.

# Eldoria and simulation research bridge

**Non-canon development research.** This file grants no authority to change lore,
promote tentative material, simulate metaphysics or recreate established events.
The [Eldoria index](../ELDORIA_INDEX.md) defines authority. Dedicated documents and
their section-level statuses outrank this navigation aid. All canonical files remain
unchanged by this program. Scientific findings are not fictional facts; fictional
facts do not command experimental outcomes.

## How research may inform lore

A demonstrated constraint can motivate a worldbuilding question:020 missed donor
help suggests asking what ordinary opportunity costs a culture's obligations impose.
It does not establish that Oceanids use caches, queue by ID or share food according
to hunger scores.021 shows that retaining an old environmental observation can
help or waste real time while later material states reconverge;this suggests asking
how inhabitants legitimately learn that a place has changed. It supplies no
Oceanus memory,Stillwater access,universal curiosity rule or fictional psychology.011's negative result cautions against assuming cheap questions
produce knowledge; it does not define fictional curiosity. A negative or repetitive
simulation outcome can expose a missing causal model without invalidating canon.

When a future result challenges a lore assumption, record the exact tested scope,
section-level canon status and conflict for user discussion. Offer alternatives
as speculation. Never silently revise established lore or treat experimental
calibration as an authoritative measurement of fictional people.

## Prospective game-support outlook

The read-only History Explorer could support NPC personal histories, relationship
changes, settlement chronologies, background social histories and persistent world
state inspection after those mechanisms exist. Current typed source links and
actor/observer separation are useful foundations. Missing experience remains unknown;
adjacent timestamps do not justify motives or causation. No quests, generated
dialogue, procedural civilizations, live game controls or story generator are
implemented or authorized by this bridge.

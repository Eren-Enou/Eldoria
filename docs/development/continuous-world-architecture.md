## Risks / gate

Rolling eligibility may create more futile contact, delay meals or hide no physical
scarcity. Reporting period controls are a treatment contrast, not all possibilities
of continuous life. Short null and long exhaustion are mandatory. Strictly separate
selected action, consent, admission, execution and material change. Existing
atomic inquiry can still dominate timing. Mechanically generated host adds build
maintenance cost; parity and source identity are required. Preserve failed outcomes.
The separate package must remain experimental, without foundation/default adoption.

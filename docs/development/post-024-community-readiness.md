# Community readiness after Experiment 024

Experiment 024 is accepted with qualification as a causal activity-valuation
experiment. MealRelief is unadopted, opt-in research; the protected foundation
ends at 023. See the [complete results](../../experiments/024/results.md),
[independent findings](../../experiments/024/independent-findings.json) and
[verification](../../experiments/024/verification.md). Original ordinals below
are zero-based. Repeated deterministic fixtures are not statistical populations.

1. **Sensitivity to own physical need?** Demonstrated within the implemented
   hunger/food model. Consume already depends on own H; adding min(H,40) changes
   relative selection in 19 of 27 pairs, followed by actual meals and persistent
   physical differences. This does not establish understanding of health or
   survival: hunger is capped and the world has no death or health model.

2. **Does social contact still matter?** Yes. Treatment still permits many
   independent invitations, acceptances and transfers, and 19 pairs change final
   directed trust. Ordinal 1 has 29 scenes and 16 transfers after its earlier meals.
   Contact can redistribute food or deliver information; counts do not establish
   that every scene is beneficial. Exhaustion 18/19 changes 87→4 transfers while
   consuming the final two otherwise circulating units.

3. **Are changes locally explained?** The full reader checks captured actor inputs,
   legal candidates and exact scores; a complementary reader checks the same
   arithmetic independently. All 27 matched pre-choice prefixes pass. The new
   selector sees own H and existing candidates, not future utility, private
   partners, scenario labels or observer outcomes. Scratch leak controls reject
   violations. Later results remain observer explanations of consequences.

4. **Is owned food still ignored?** Yes at lower hunger. Across the 27 treatment
   worlds there are 540 eligible nonselected Consume decisions; an actual-time
   subset is exposed in the viewer. None occurs at H100 in this registered
   treatment matrix. That empirical absence is narrower than a guarantee:
   the pure control retains Invite176 above treatment Consume140 under a
   legitimately captured earlier strong-contact input.

5. **Excessive survival focus?** There is no absolute eat-first rule. The costly
   0/1 comparison nevertheless shows that extra immediate priority can consume
   earlier, alter encounters and end with fewer total meals and higher hunger
   areas. This is evidence against assuming that immediate relief deserves
   universal priority, not evidence of simulated survival motivation.

6. **Do personal histories affect later choices?** Existing directed trust and
   retained last-four episode contributions still influence contact scores.
   Both undergo legitimate later changes. A meal-induced timing divergence
   interacts with these mechanisms; 024 does not isolate their separate mediation
   effects or prove that every later event is uniquely caused by one earlier unit.
   Episode eviction does not erase separately established trust or permit archive
   recovery of unavailable cognition.

7. **Are resource limits binding?** Strongly. No policy creates food. Exhaustion
   treatment consumes all five finite units and eventually all four actors reach
   H100. The 768-horizon extension preserves a finite cumulative relief difference,
   then records continuing empty encounters. Cache depletion and exhaustion of all
   owned food are distinct events.

8. **Can four people continue without report-window shutdown?** Within declared
   finite evaluation, yes: commitments cross reporting checkpoints and independent
   actors continue. All primitive clock/reservation/deadline checks and report
   placebos pass. New choices still stop at the nominal cutoff, and existing work
   completes with a charged 33-tick drain. This is not an indefinite live service.

9. **More or fewer distinct trajectories?** Nineteen pairs change executed histories
   and final trust; eight have behavioral/state nulls apart from score fields.
   Three temporarily reconverge in food accounting. Actors' hunger-area effects
   can differ in sign within one pair. These are identifiable distinct histories,
   not a measure of personality diversity or a monotonic increase in emergence.

10. **Does complexity improve understanding?** The four scalar term records make
    the intervention auditable and show both relief and opportunity cost. They
    occupy about 2.11% of decoded output without increasing cognitive capacity.
    This modest mechanism isolates a question; it does not calibrate activity
    scores or justify adoption. Large captured observer histories remain costly.

11. **Which behavior remains artificial?** Independently scaled contact and meal
    scores, capped hunger, repeated empty encounters, coarse actor profiles,
    atomic inquiry, comparison-phase timing and conservative reservation bounds.
    Adding a constant-linked heuristic does not remove those modeling choices.
    Incoming consent can still occupy someone before an ordinary meal decision.

12. **What is underused?** Inquiry has no treatment effect in these fixtures. All
    ten queries remain Direct, with native null/provenance receipt distinctions
    and the same partial retained progress. Evidence inquiry, richer source
    learning and valuation of uncertain delayed work were not stress-tested.
    Finite food circulation can dominate contact without improving nutritional use.

13. **What could justify later adoption?** A separately authorized prospective
    comparison with fixed criteria for physical, social and information tradeoffs;
    broader independent fixtures; clear opportunity-cost tests; meaningful nulls
    and harmful cases; exact replay/local reconstruction; and manageable recording
    cost. Do not choose a policy solely by total meals, scene count or aggregate
    hunger. A separate scientific review/integrity pass must precede protection.

14. **Ready for productive material work?** Ready for designing a small, separately
    authorized finite preparation study, with valuation retained as an explicit
    confound and possible cause of non-use. The scheduler/accounting boundary is
    a defensible starting point, not proof that individuals can value delayed
    preparation. No raw material, preparation action or new supply exists in 024.
    See the [future handoff](post-024-material-preparation-handoff.md).

Activity valuation remains an important limitation, but the evidence no longer
supports treating all food neglect as a scheduling failure. The immediate research
question is how locally known effort and delayed yield compete with useful contact
and current consumption, while preserving non-use and costly outcomes. This does
not require more people, an economy, language, institutions or a general planner.

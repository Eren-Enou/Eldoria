## Part C — Experiment 024 design

consumption, social and environmental consequences under otherwise unchanged

public-duration pricing, retained contact feedback and combinations. The latter
three were deferred to avoid changing cognition, interpreting all zero transfers
as failure or confounding several valuation mechanisms. One primary intervention

existing40-point meal effect. Other scores, eligibility, ties, source/consent,

The pure function receives existing own Input and four candidate records after
inherited protocol masks. No metadata label, future success, observer history or
private partner state enters it. Incoming recipient decisions remain untouched
and can reserve an actor before an ordinary choice. Consume/Acquire are legally

history. No three-way eligibility was fabricated by changing old laws.

scarcity, nulls, reconvergence and potential harm. The reporting sequence and

sensitivity, not independent statistical evidence. No retuning followed outcomes.

## Part F — Long-term development and remaining uncertainty

The [14-question readiness assessment](post-024-community-readiness.md) concludes
that valuation is still an important limitation, alongside finite scarcity and
the absence of productive affordances. Continuous scheduling remains reliable
within its actual bounded/reservation/drain semantics. Different histories affect
later scores and consent, but the study does not isolate every causal mediator.
No universal policy ranking or realistic, viable community is established.

The [future handoff](post-024-material-preparation-handoff.md) reviews the prior
finite-input design. A separately authorized delayed-work experiment is justified
as a question, not as a guaranteed next implementation. Tentative2 raw→1 edible+
1 waste must preserve every physical compartment, actual effort/extraction/
preparation, in-process ownership, finite stock and later voluntary use. Keep
non-use, failed effort, missed help, output never used and harmful outcomes.
Require new prospective gates/controls; do not tune024 to guarantee preparation.
No renewable supply, farms, professions, markets, currency, law, institutions,
planner,025 registration or preparation code belongs to this delivery.

Strongest unresolved question: can a transparent local policy compare the
opportunity cost of immediate relief with uncertain but locally known delayed
physical work and useful independent social contact, without learning from
unavailable counterfactual outcomes? 024 identifies the conflict; it does not solve
it. That is the scientific reason to preserve both costly and useful choices.

## Minimum next milestone and stop gates

The highest physical priority is a finite input that can be voluntarily converted
at real cost into later edible material, with waste and competition accounted.
The [prospective design](sustained-material-opportunity-design.md) is design only.
Before implementation, independently review022, decide which findings are worth
protecting, resolve timing semantics prospectively and authorize a new study. Do
not automatically extend001–021 or begin023.

A minimum continuously operating milestone would additionally require multiple
material cycles with explicit source/energy limits, no resets, lawful uninterrupted
availability, legitimate local observations, voluntary alternatives/consent,
persistent distinct histories, independent conservation/replay and inspectable
failed/unequal outcomes. It must tolerate depletion and failure rather than force
everyone to survive. Reopened public opportunity windows and interleaved queries
need separate causal tests. A larger population or more transfers cannot substitute.

The next authorized development session should first review022 and the disclosed
phase/horizon/query limitations. If accepted, implement only the finite conversion
boundary after prospective specification and controls. The strongest open question
is whether the existing contact priorities voluntarily allocate enough time to
delayed material work for later use, rather than ignoring the opportunity or
merely shifting meals into the closing tail. Preserve a negative answer.

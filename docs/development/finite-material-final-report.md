## Part G — Community readiness

A real finite, costly productive activity now exists and is voluntarily selected.
Finished output is optionally used, contacts compete with work/use, scarcity binds,
and individuals accumulate different physical/cognitive/social histories. This is
a meaningful additional causal capability, not a self-maintaining community.

The [readiness assessment](post-025-community-readiness.md) classifies all15K1
capabilities and ranks L1 bottlenecks: A valuation, B finite inputs, D information,
E scheduling, C materially empty contact, G audit/architecture, F coupling, H
remaining transfer limits. This evidence-weighted priority is not universal utility.
The most justified next question concerns bounded local valuation of preparing,
consuming and sharing; explicit physical renewal would require its own ontology
and authorization. No next experiment was begun.

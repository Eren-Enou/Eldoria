## Phase 3 — Four individuals and simultaneous independent work

First test four, not a large population. Question: can two disjoint encounters and
other independently admitted work proceed under one clock without double booking,
global knowledge or scripted matching? Include competing claims, unequal durations,
co-presence changes, declined invitations, simultaneous deadlines and atomic-query
conflicts. Compare serial and concurrent execution without changing policies.

Exit evidence: actor-specific busy intervals, actual consent, deterministic legal
arbitration, no skipped deadlines, conservation, local mirrored inputs and exact
replay. Measure observer/runtime cost at four before making scalability claims.
Do not automatically replace the scheduler. Extract shared lifecycle code only
after its ownership/semantic equivalence can be demonstrated against frozen paths.

## Phase 4 — Sustained material opportunities

Finite cache depletion cannot support indefinite consumption. A prospective physical
conversion process must declare inputs, source stocks, conversion ratios, effort,
time, access, ownership, loss and exhaustion. Include cases where conversion is
unavailable, unprofitable or loses a better social opportunity. A replenishment
timer introduced solely to avoid saturation would not establish a coherent process.

Exit evidence: several non-reset cycles with an explicit extended conservation
ledger, measurable input depletion/limits, independent activity selection and
legitimate information. Do not label a generic conversion process farming or an
economy without the additional supporting model and evidence. Keep scarcity/failure.

## Phase 5 — Distinct personal trajectories

Use common rules and differing actual experience rather than scripted personality
roles. Ask whether resource histories change later opportunities, directed trust
still matters, misunderstandings persist, information can be corrected and bad
expectations can recover. Separate inherited initial traits from learned effects.

Controls: matched experiences before a defined divergence, history ablation with
material state preserved, private-source twins, alternative future opportunities,
long enough follow-up for persistence/reconvergence. More varied traces alone do
not establish meaningful individuality or psychological realism.

## Phase 6 — Minimum viable continuously operating community

A bounded milestone: a small stable population, real physical inputs/resources,
several legitimate changing activities, shared time, independent choices/consent,
local incomplete knowledge, exchange and persistent social/material history over
several meaningful cycles without resets. Record failure, inequity and mundane life.

Measure sustained opportunities, time spent productively/unproductively, individual
resource/need histories, causal relevance of prior encounters and observational
cost. Compare alternative futures; do not impose a target number of friendships,
transfers or successful survivors. Technical operation is not proof that a
believable community, culture or civilization has emerged.

## Phase 7 — Social organization, only after continuous life is credible

Investigate persistent cooperative arrangements, reciprocity, shared resource rules,
conflict mediation, informal authority, norms, membership and specialization one
mechanism at a time. An obligation must be represented in an individual's legitimate
state and affect actual choices; an observer label is insufficient. Institutions
need maintenance, communication and possible collapse, not a global kingdom flag.
No predetermined hierarchy, law, market, war or shared culture is required.

Exit evidence: arrangements persist through particular individual acts, can fail,
and change later opportunities beyond what simpler local feedback explains. No
institutional implementation is authorized by this roadmap.

## Phase 8 — Observable history at several scales

Individual: what was experienced, retained, selected and executed?
Relationship: how did actual encounters and interpretations change the pair?
Community: how did access, allocation and consent affect different inhabitants?
Historical: which persistent changes altered later possibilities?

Extend read-only Explorer adapters with exact source references and clear unknowns.
Recorded summaries may eventually support NPC histories, settlement chronology,
background simulation and explanation of changed relationships. These are prospective
game-support uses, not quests, dialogue generation or live simulation control.
Prose must present verified history; it must never cause that history.

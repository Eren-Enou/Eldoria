including successful sharing,full-cost empty claims,missed donor help and later
depleted-world repetition. Known-empty exclusion remains structural;its unwitnessed
provisional atomic-query blocking branch remains a limitation,not empirical coverage.

universal memory model. LastSeen changes43/57 matched activity selections,14 period

grant→meal/social execution→later state;another actual chain includes a rival's
intervening grant and a later voluntary transfer. Stale claims,redistribution,
ordering reversal,expiry loss and useless extra work survive in the evidence.

## Community-readiness answer and next session

The project is closer in a specific sense:finite material opportunity,owned partial
environmental information,persistent social/material consequences and inspectable
recorded histories now work together under one clock. It still cannot support a
small continuously functioning community. Finite supply,one stationary source,three
actors,one resource pair,atomic query,fixed windows,passive sensing and old valuation
heuristics are substantial constraints. Audit growth and duplicated lifecycle code
also limit expansion. No sustainable economy,active exploration or general social
emergence is demonstrated.

Next:independently review021's scoring,matched prefixes,actual traces,raw-versus-
semantic projections,negative controls and passive-sensing confound. Challenge
whether information continuity is worth its cost versus simpler sensing. A separate
integrity/adoption decision may follow only after review. Do not automatically
protect021,start022,add institutions or rewrite canon. Production,travel,markets,
language generation and story generation remain prospective work requiring evidence.

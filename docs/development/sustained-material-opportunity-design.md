# Prospective finite-input material opportunity

**Design only.** No023implementation, numerical experiment matrix, new source
archive, policy adoption or fictional canon is authorized here. This proposes a
small physical extension to investigate after independent022review and explicit
authorization. It is not a claim of renewable food or an indefinitely sustainable
community. [Readiness assessment](four-individual-community-readiness.md) explains
why more people are not yet the next step.

## Limitation to address

The022world can redistribute finite food, acquire finite cache units and consume
them. It cannot create later material opportunities from any other input. In
ongoing32/33 all12units are consumed. More concurrent contact changes timing and
distribution but creates no additional food; even longer histories can become
repeated empty encounters. A replenishment timer would hide this limitation.

Question for a future authorized study: can an individual voluntarily spend scarce
time on a finite, locally known preparation opportunity, obtain edible material,
then actually use or transfer it with persistent consequences while others pursue
independent work? Retain outcomes where preparation is ignored, fails, wastes a
better opportunity or never leads to consumption. The question is causal use,
not optimal scheduling, economic growth or collective survival.

## Smallest physical ontology

Use one stationary, publicly accessible finite stock of generic **unprepared
nutrient-bearing material**, declared in initial conditions with an explicit
source identity. It is material already present, not newly spawned food. Avoid
species, crops, farms, hunting, tools or professions. The existing individuals
remain co-present; access grants physical opportunity, not a legal property right.

Physical compartments would be:

- Unprepared stock at the source, in indivisible mass units.
- Actor-owned material held in one in-process escrow during preparation.
- Finished edible units in the existing owned-food inventory.
- Inert waste, retained in an observer physical ledger.
- Cumulative consumed material, removed from usable ownership.
- Existing finite ready-food cache, if present in controls.

Keep source stock and escrow amounts bounded integer quantities with checked
arithmetic. At most one batch per committed actor; four actors remain the maximum.
No free raw-material trading or second inventory economy is needed initially.
Waste has physical persistence but no new use or cognitive subsystem. Finished
food enters the existing Offer/Accept/Refuse/Consume rules without changing them.

## Candidate conversion law and accounting

An illustrative law is **2 unprepared mass units → 1 edible unit + 1 waste unit**.
This is a concrete candidate ratio for design review, not a preregistered numerical
matrix or tuned yield. Interpret the raw material as containing the edible
fraction already. Preparation separates that fraction; it does not create matter
or nutrients from nothing. State this ontology before fixing future coefficients.

Declare one common accounting mass per unit for this proposed law. At every
operation, including mid-process:

`M = raw_source + sum(raw_escrow) + ready_cache + sum(owned_food) + waste + consumed`

`M` is constant after initial conditions. A claim moves2source units into owned
escrow; conversion removes2escrow and credits1food plus1waste; transfer debits and
credits the same edible unit; eating debits food and credits consumed. Depleted
failure moves no material. Observations and promises move no material. No account
may go negative, wrap, overfill or silently discard a remainder.

The established40-point hunger reduction is an abstract need response, not a
caloric conservation equation. This minimal design does not claim complete human
metabolism or a physically detailed energy budget. Mechanical/biological work is
represented by occupied time and recurring need. If a later proposal chemically
creates energy, increases nutrient potential, or renews stock, it must explicitly
model its external energy/material source and losses first. This proposal does
none of those things. Both raw and edible stocks eventually exhaust.

## One voluntary activity, two physical phases

Prefer one bounded **Prepare** commitment rather than a planner or an elaborate
chain of raw-item goals. Its local candidate competes with existing contact,
Consume, Acquire, Query and Wait. Do not auto-start preparation on hunger, schedule
it by scenario name or command a person to produce the useful output.

1. Admission checks actor availability, declared duration, inventory/output room
   and legitimate public access. It reserves the actor alone for the whole activity.
   Actual source stock is not disclosed by admission or consulted by the policy.
2. After a declared extraction effort interval, the resolver checks actual stock.
   If enough exists, debit one batch atomically and credit that actor's process
   escrow. Concurrent extraction uses the same publicly declared deterministic
   ordering rule; one batch cannot be claimed twice.
3. A second positive preparation interval occupies the same reservation. On
   success, debit escrow and credit food/waste with the declared ratio. Food is
   unavailable for transfer or consumption before this actual completion.
4. If extraction was depleted, no escrow/output is created. Preserve the full
   committed effort/time cost through completion, audit the reason, and release
   exactly once. A failure receipt may legitimately update the actor's currently
   retained local source observation under explicitly declared rules.
5. Only after release may the actor voluntarily eat, transfer, inquire or do
   something else. A later edible unit is an opportunity, not a scripted meal.

This proposal does not need intrinsic stochastic spoilage or an interruption
action. Its initial physical failure is depleted/insufficient input, and ordinary
waste occurs on every success. Adding quality failures, cancellation, injuries or
refunds would require new physical properties, legitimate cues and exact escrow
settlement rules before implementation. Do not add them merely to decorate traces.
Shutdown/export must preserve in-process escrow rather than credit premature food.

## Scheduling and opportunity cost

Use a versioned opt-in successor boundary after022review. Current022source freezes
include host files: do not add a Work variant in place and silently alter original
serialization or old replay. Locate a legal additive integration point, document
its ownership, and prove historical disabled-path equivalence first. No new code
is part of this design delivery.

Preparation must share the actual four-person clock and exclusive actor reservation
with existing work. Disjoint resource scenes, claims and meals continue while a
person prepares. Need accrues for everyone once per elapsed interval; another
actor cannot both reply and prepare. Competing extraction deadlines resolve in
fixed order, with debits before subsequent grants. Phase ownership must explicitly
specify same-tick need, scenes, extraction, completion, meals and release. No
observer-derived ordering, extra ticks or skipped deadlines is permissible.

Atomic query remains conservative unless independently redesigned: no jump over
preparation/extraction deadlines or passive intermediate choices. Existing public
closing budgets can make delayed work ineligible or produce artificial end tails.
Resolve absolute versus comparison-relative pulse/sensing epochs prospectively;
022did not establish phase invariance. Do not claim a continuous community from
windows that merely reset budgets. A future study must state admission/drain/end
semantics before outcomes and inspect both cumulative and terminal need.

## Actor-local information and bounded choice

An individual may use its own food/hunger, current public availability/access,
known effort/yield rules, owned retained source observation and dated own take
adjustment. It may not see current hidden stock, rival escrows, rival inventories,
future partners, future need usefulness, actual unreceived quality or observer
history. Missing observations remain unknown. Failed extraction is new local
evidence, not archive recovery. Separate trust/concerns can persist after episode
eviction under their existing contracts.

Prefer a tagged Food/Unprepared observation in the existing **single environmental
slot**, rather than secretly increasing capacity. Receiving one can overwrite
the other. The person may consequently forget a useful opportunity or make a
stale claim. Design source identity, receipt namespace and own-removal behavior
explicitly; no raw observer provenance should enter activity scoring. A process
status needed to finish committed work is bounded physical bookkeeping, not
permission to reconstruct evicted stock knowledge.

The new Prepare candidate needs a pure, replaceable and independently testable
local heuristic, declared before the future matrix. Preserve old candidate scores
and ties; do not choose weights because they force a preparation/meal chain.
Public effort/yield rules are legitimate affordance knowledge, not forecast actual
success. Expected usefulness and realized benefit must be measured separately.
If existing contact scores crowd preparation out, report that negative result
rather than injecting automatic actions or a general planner.

## Proposed comparisons, without a numerical registration

Hold initial physical stocks, profiles, seeds, knowledge, source access, clocks,
partners and external opportunities fixed until the first voluntary divergence.
Compare a disabled conversion affordance with an explicitly enabled one; raw
material remains physically present in both. This estimates access to a process,
not superior personality or an equal-initial-edibility comparison. A ready-food
placement control changes initial accessibility and must be labeled a separate
physical counterfactual, not a matched policy effect.

Useful controls include sufficient versus depleted raw input, full versus stale
observation, low versus high declared effort, a genuine competing social
opportunity, different deterministic contention order, delayed versus early
availability, no legitimate candidate, and identical relevant local inputs despite
different hidden stocks. Include no-use, failed claim, successful output without
later use, useful transfer/meal, costly/missed help and reconvergence cases. Do not
arrange every later opportunity around whichever activity the actor happened to
select. Fix future schedules independently of mode; keep failed outcomes.

Evaluate parameter sensitivity only after mechanism/scenarios are specified.
No world count, seeds, durations, thresholds, population expansion or future study
matrix is registered here. Exact deterministic reruns are reproducibility,
not independent statistical samples. Retention/inquiry/defaults remain unchanged.

## Expected evidence and validation burden

Require the whole executed chain: local candidate input → scored voluntary choice
→ admission/reservation → actual source debit and escrow → timed conversion and
waste → release → later voluntary resource action/meal → subsequent persistent
state. Observe at least several actual material cycles without state resets where
they arise; do not count repeated unproductive probes as cycles. Attribute missed
help and costs without claiming an unexecuted alternative would certainly succeed.

An independent primitive reader must reconstruct every compartment and operation,
integer scores/ties, own receipts, actor occupancy, both deadlines, need, consent,
later transfer/meal and final state. Reject scratch duplicate extraction, invisible
stock leakage, premature output, lost escrow, double release, missing waste,
negative stock, overflow, time jump, double booking, false consent and history
recovery. Keep originals immutable with source locks and exact replay. Run the
actual protected foundation plus022compatibility, Rust checks and relevant cost
measurements. Scientific gates must not depend on presentation caches.

Extend the four-lane observer to show input→escrow→food/waste references, keeping
actor perspective captured-only. Measure cost of longer preparation histories and
observer capture separately from bounded runtime state. Numerical coefficients
and schema should be reviewable before any future implementation.

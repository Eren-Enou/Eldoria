## Ranked barriers by demonstrated causal importance

1. **Finite supply eventually dominates every food history.**020 ongoing consumes
   all available36 units and reaches hunger100; empty contacts continue. Access
   moves depletion165→213 versus Closed, not sustainability. A material conversion
   model is ultimately necessary, but adding replenishment now would obscure the
   information/opportunity question and needs an independently justified design.
2. **Opportunity information is unusually fresh.**020 every free decision sees
   current stock; known-zero exclusion is structural. It cannot test whether
   legitimately remembered environmental information matters, misleads, expires or
   gets refreshed. This is a small isolatable next question motivating021.
3. **Opportunity value is weakly grounded.**01910% score changes are usually
   terminally inert yet sometimes reverse individual outcomes;020 Need can prefer
   empty contact, and acquisition can lose a willing donor. Trust/contact yield/
   stock expectation/time cost use different units. Do not fix this by tuning
   scores in021; separate information availability from valuation.
4. **Scheduling cannot yet represent independent groups.** Three actors allow one
   pair plus one free worker. Atomic inquiry blocks interior choices and cache
   claims.020's562-line copied lifecycle exposes drift risk. Four people/two
   disjoint encounters are the next architectural probe after a narrow knowledge
   study, not permission to replace the scheduler wholesale.
5. **Externally bounded opportunities shape behavior.** Evidence queries need33
   reserved ticks and never fit02024/4 windows.019 demonstrates a real four-tick
   transfer excluded by public bound8. Future opportunity continuity must compare
   physical time independently of window index; hidden future durations cannot
   justify looser retrospective admission.
6. **No spatial or renewable physical setting.** Everyone is co-present at one
   cache. Multiple places, movement and production require separate physical
   conservation/locality questions. Institutions, families and markets cannot
   substitute for these prerequisites.
7. **Audit cost limits practical observation.**020 raw607MB/164 worlds, original
   single workload execution about2.17×019 for matching Owned behavior. Derived
   selected-trial tools help observation, not physics or cognitive scalability.

Ranks separate urgency for eventual continuous life from order of feasible causal
experiments. Supply is the largest long-run barrier; bounded observation is the
smallest next clean information test. Neither preference predicts a favorable result.

A future minimum milestone should execute several material/social cycles without
state resets, with possible failure, inequity and misunderstanding. Passing an
interface checklist alone would establish an operating model, not believable social
emergence. No consciousness, personhood or calibrated realism claim follows.

## Order of work and gates

Existing cognition is not a generic environmental database. `model::Memory` stores
resource-event/partner/action/interpretation/valence;002 Belief and007 Knowledge are
event-specific social support;008 Item is a boolean claim with communicator,
quality and typed received origin. None represents a measured cache quantity/time
without inventing a social event or changing old assessment semantics. Reusing their
caps alone would not make that substitution legitimate. Any environmental record
must therefore justify its own narrow ownership/receipt/lifetime boundary while
leaving those protected stores unchanged. A static observation audit alone cannot
serve as actor memory.

Independently review/protect020; integrate its authentic main-viewer histories;
prospectively isolate environmental observation continuity in021. Compare free
legitimate sensing at declared opportunities against retained last-seen state,
unknown state and a simple non-memory control under identical food/opportunities.
Do not combine new sensing action, scheduler and valuation in that treatment.

Then investigate four-person disjoint scheduling, justified material conversion,
and history-dependent trajectories under changing alternatives. Institutional or
game-facing features remain later research. The separately planned roadmap will define further evidence gates;the canon
research bridge grants no authority to alter fictional history or metaphysics.

Strongest open question: does information continuity contribute behavior beyond a
current sensor rule, or merely create avoidable wasted work under the existing
heuristics? Preserve either answer and the costs of the mechanism.

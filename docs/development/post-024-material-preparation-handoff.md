# Future finite material-preparation handoff after 024

**Recommendation only.** No Experiment 025 is registered or implemented. No
material-production system or new supply was added by this program. The earlier
[finite-input proposal](sustained-material-opportunity-design.md) remains tentative;
its historical authorization boundary is not changed in place.

## What 024 changes in the rationale

The continuous scheduler and finite accounting survived exact reconstruction of
56 additional worlds. Local ordinary valuation can change actual meals, later
encounters and persistent ownership/trust. A small hunger-linked term reduces
owned-food neglect in particular fixtures, but paired 0/1 yields fewer meals and
higher hunger for several actors. Exhaustion consumes all five units under the
treatment, then still reaches H100 for everyone. Preparation should investigate a
new finite affordance, not conceal an unresolved valuation problem by replenishing
food. See [results](../../experiments/024/results.md) and the
[readiness assessment](post-024-community-readiness.md).

Valuation remains a research limitation rather than a categorical blocker to a
bounded study. Preserving the possibility of ignoring a locally known work
opportunity is scientifically useful. Neither Baseline nor MealRelief is a
validated general utility system; do not adopt or retune either to guarantee work.

## Candidate physical boundary requiring separate authorization

The prior proposal's illustrative conversion, **2 raw units → 1 edible + 1 waste**,
is a possible separation of an already present edible fraction. It is not a fixed
yield or a claim to create matter or nutrients. A future design must declare the
unit ontology, finite initial stock and exact conversion before outcomes.

Separate physical compartments would be finite source stock, bounded actor-owned
in-process escrow, existing ready-food cache, owned edible food, persistent waste
and consumed material. A candidate conservation equation is:

`M = raw_source + sum(raw_escrow) + ready_cache + sum(owned_food) + waste + consumed`

Extraction moves material into escrow; preparation debits escrow and credits food
and waste exactly; later transfer/consumption uses established accounting. None of
these compartments or operations is implemented here. Finished food must not be
available during extraction or before actual preparation completion.

The smallest future affordance could remain one voluntary Prepare commitment
with two positive-duration phases, extraction and preparation, at most one batch
per actor. Preserve expensive depletion failure and success without later use.
Do not force a later meal, gift or cooperation chain. A reservation is physical
bookkeeping, not a planner or newly remembered source knowledge.

## What must remain unchanged

- Protected 001–023 source/evidence and original 024 evidence; additive versioned
  successor integration, with disabled-path exact equivalence before treatment.
- Four actors, actual shared elapsed time, independent choice and recipient consent.
  Do not let preparation skip another person's deadline, inquiry exclusion or need
  charge. Declare same-tick phase ownership and ties prospectively.
- Existing food/consumption/negotiation rules and no automatic output use. Keep the
  nominal stop and charged completion semantics explicit; unfinished escrow must
  persist or settle by a preregistered physical rule, never become premature food.
- Existing episodic, concern, evidence and environmental limits. Prefer the prior
  single tagged environmental slot over a second unacknowledged source-memory
  capacity. Dated receipts, own takes and legitimate new failure receipts remain
  distinct from current hidden stock. Forgetting cannot be repaired from archives.
- Actor-local scoring, deterministic stable ties, transparent public effort/yield
  affordances, bounded arithmetic and no hidden future or partner-private inputs.
  Do not expose rival escrow or actual future extraction success to policy.

Current 024 ordinary reservations use a declared maximum horizon bound. Future
two-phase work must fit a justified bound or explicitly justify a new versioned
bound; it cannot silently extend frozen 023/024 deadlines. Its pure candidate
heuristic would need a separate hypothesis and design gate. This handoff supplies
no policy coefficients, numerical matrix, world count or experiment registration.

## Independent controls required in a future proposal

Compare enabled versus disabled access to the same finite material under fixed
initial states, policy, knowledge, seeds and opportunity sequence. Keep a policy
contrast distinct from an affordance contrast. Ready edible food placed initially
is a separate physical counterfactual, not an equal-accessibility policy control.
If Baseline and MealRelief are included, retain their exact 024 laws and label
their opt-in status; do not change them while introducing the new work action.

Include known versus unavailable observations, stale versus current receipts,
sufficient versus depleted stock, competition and deterministic extraction order,
short versus costly effort, a genuine useful social alternative, nominal-boundary
crossing, failure after time spent, output never consumed/shared, useful later use,
costly missed help, nulls and reconvergence. Fix later schedules independently of
what actors choose. A locally identical decision must not change merely because
hidden source stock or a future partner differs.

Independent reconstruction must cover every material compartment and phase,
ownership/exclusion, need pulses, receipt lifetimes, scores/ties, source identities,
actual extraction/preparation success or failure, waste, later voluntary resource
use and persistent effects. Scratch corruption should target double extraction,
phantom output, missing waste, early food access, uncharged time, lost escrow,
private/future leakage and observer recovery. Replays and source/archive hashes
remain necessary, alongside meaningful positive, costly and null classifications.

## Invalidating shortcuts and remaining question

A future study is invalid if the controller starts work, rescues hunger by new
supply, adjusts weights after outcomes, scripts the later recipient, uses actual
hidden stock to choose, credits output early, drops failed histories, equates more
food or gifts with universal benefit, or labels deterministic seed replication
independent statistical evidence. Renewables, farms, professions, jobs, markets,
currency, law, institutions and population expansion are outside this recommendation.

The strongest unresolved question is whether the individual can voluntarily value
a **locally known but uncertain delayed physical result** against a useful social
opportunity, incur its real cost, and later decide what to do with actual output.
024 establishes sensitivity to immediate relief, not that delayed-work capability.
A new study needs separate authorization, prospective registration and its own
scientific gate before any implementation.

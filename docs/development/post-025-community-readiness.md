# After025: requirements for persistent community life

This assesses the named StageL objective using the completed025evidence. The supplied
program text ends in StageI3; unseen detailed StageJ–L instructions and final delivery
requirements have been requested and cannot be certified by this document. It does
not authorize026, protect025, adopt a policy or change Eldoria canon.

## What the project now demonstrates

Four persistent individuals can encounter a finite material source, retain an owned
fallible upper bound, choose delayed work, compete for actual stock, hold raw escrow,
convert it into food/waste, and later choose meals or social exchange. Other actors
can work concurrently. Failed extraction costs real occupied time and advancing
needs. Waste, inventory, social history and local knowledge remain separate.

The narrow causal result is supported by50registered worlds,24matched access pairs,
2report placebos,21pairs with changed execution, independent reconstruction,
22corruption rejections and exact replay. It is not supported merely by a new action
enum or a successful policy probe. See [results](../../experiments/025/results.md),
[review](../experiment-025-review.md) and [verification](../../experiments/025/verification.md).

## Why this is not yet a productive community

| Requirement | Current evidence and gap |
|---|---|
| Conserved usable-material supply | Finite2raw→1food+1waste works. Source stock never renews; initial material remains an externally specified finite condition. |
| Material usefulness | Actual meals/transfers occur. More prepared units or more transfers are not equivalent to lower hunger for everyone. |
| Bounded evaluation of work | `H − known duration` is a replaceable local heuristic. It does not estimate future meal value, competing contacts or allocation welfare. |
| Information under competition | Rival removals can invalidate retained upper bounds. Fresh failure receipts legitimately correct knowledge, after costs have already been paid. No coordinated source-reservation knowledge is supplied. |
| Voluntary later use | Meals and social choices are independently executed. Five unused outputs have no later ordinary meal opportunity; persistent voluntary neglect is not demonstrated. |
| Social allocation | Record17donates a prepared unit to an actor already holding2food. Actor2's access-enabled hunger area is worse. This does not establish a transfer-specific counterfactual, economic efficiency or stable mutual support. |
| Temporal continuity | Work crosses reports and nominal cutoffs with charged drain. An evaluation horizon still ends ordinary opportunities; this is not an indefinitely operating settlement. |
| Physiological consistency | Hunger progresses with time and meals. It caps at100and does not model death, energy feasibility, fatigue or health. No biological survival inference is justified. |
| Long-term material cycles | Waste is persistent inert mass. No reuse, source replenishment, energy flows, perishability or ecological process exists. |
| Population and diversity | Four actors, fixed profiles, one stationary source and controlled opportunities. There is no evidence of population-scale labor organization or naturally arising institutions. |
| Historical understanding | Original inputs/operations and typed causal links support inspection. Observer archives remain distinct from current cognition; source namespace and JSON transport defects required transparent corrections. |
| Measured complexity | Cognitive/source/work caps hold. Observer history is large; final-snapshot copying and verification have costs. Research-only observer overhead is not evidence of a scalable living-world runtime. |

## Development boundaries justified by the evidence

Preserve finite losses, misleading local expectations, costly sharing and nulls.
Do not repair behavior simply because a reviewer can see unused stock or a better
future. A later correction cannot refund spent work, consumed food or executed
relationship history. Losing a source cell must not erase separately established
physical/social consequences; those consequences must not restore forgotten source
knowledge from observer archives.

Before claiming sustainable community life, the project would need explicit,
testable answers about material inputs/cycles, the meaning of physical effort and
need, and how local decisions can maintain useful activity over continuing uncertain
opportunities. A scheduled free refill would not by itself answer those questions.
Neither a general planner nor institutions, factions, markets, travel or language
generation is a consequence of passing025.

The strongest evidence-grounded question is how an individual should value preparing,
consuming and sharing when an apparently reasonable local choice can worsen its
later condition. Existing novelty/progress learning and current contact scores are
not already an answer. This is an open question for future authorization, not a new
experiment specification or implementation in this program.

## B — Intervention

Only eligible Prepare valuation changes:

```
D = own retained extraction_ticks + preparation_ticks
Baseline Prepare = H - D
ProjectedNeed Prepare = min(100, H + 5 * floor(D / 4)) - D
```

H is own current hunger. The fixed law prior uses existing own retained duration;
it contains no future outcome, observer history, private partner state or hidden
source stock. Original eligibility, four other terms, old-first strict-max ties,
physical timing, need, cognition, consent and capacities remain exact. No new
live actor state. Prepare requires food0 and Consume food>0, so they cannot be
co-eligible. The prior can overvalue long delays: projected need grows faster
than the unchanged duration penalty. This was a prospective limitation, not tuned
away. It is neither expected success nor calibrated utility.

## F — Scientific result

Demonstrated: an actor-local delay projection can alter voluntary work priorities,
completed activities and persistent material/social history. Scores frequently
change without execution; extra production and reduced failed work need not improve
individual nutrition. Benefits, harms, mixed outcomes and nulls remain visible.

Mechanism-dependent: the fixed projected-need prior, clipping/phase alignment,
old-first ties, finite stock, existing contact/inquiry and independent later use.
Ambiguous: path-specific causes of an entire hunger-area difference, overlapping
fungible consumption bounds and robustness beyond the registered sensitivities.
Unsupported: optimality, psychological realism, general social emergence,
calibrated delayed usefulness, broad superiority, survival or default adoption.
C1's domain extension and the separate post-run I1 transport repair qualify the
result; neither retroactively completes the failed original producer.

## I — Next scientific question

How should a bounded individual evaluate whether produced food will later be
consumed or shared effectively, rather than merely completed? Evidence motivating
this question is source43's worse hunger despite fewer failures, source41's mixed
extra-output result and source17's nutritionally inert circulation. A future study
would need prospective local inputs, separate learning from personally observed
material outcomes, and actual later relief/use with costs and nulls preserved.

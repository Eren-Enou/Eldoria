# Continuous-world readiness and the next material decision

**A qualified four-person continuous-opportunity prototype is achieved; a minimum
viable community is not established.** Experiment 023 remains opt-in, unprotected
and pending independent review. No 024 or material-production mechanism was started.
See [results](../../experiments/023/results.md),
[walkthrough](first-continuous-world-walkthrough.md) and the unchanged
[finite-conversion proposal](sustained-material-opportunity-design.md).

## What is ready

One Runtime clock carries the same four actors, owned cognition, food, social
history, environmental knowledge, pending consent and active commitments through
reporting periods. Disjoint social scenes overlap; claims and meals progress
alongside other legal work. Twenty original worlds have strict report crossings;
two reporting placebos preserve exact underlying histories. No observer archive
restores unavailable cognition. The source host first reproduced all 100 protected
022 originals byte-for-byte. A bounded CLI run can be independently reproduced and
inspected in the main History Explorer.

001–022 is the protected endpoint (361 artifacts). Experiment 023 is a new scheduling
result, not automatically foundation evidence or a default policy. The 022 review
accepts its implemented comparison-epoch law with qualification: original prospective
prose specified absolute phase. Prospective 023 Absolute sensitivities do not rewrite
022 or establish phase invariance.

## Remaining limitations

| Boundary | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|
| Population | Four fixed co-present people with controlled bootstrap; no natural group formation or population scaling. |
| Behavior | Unchanged contact/consumption/inquiry heuristics; continuous permission exposes costly rankings rather than improving them. |
| Inquiry | Globally exclusive atomic operation; no concurrent independent queries or physical/query interleaving. |
| Sensing | Fixed eight-tick cadence/phase and one owned LastSeen slot; no richer exploration or autonomous travel. |
| Resources | Finite ready-food cache and ownership; no later inputs, production or replenishment. |
| Needs/life | Hunger saturates at 100; no mortality, reproduction or biological sustainability model. |
| Termination | Ordinary choices stop at nominal T; an actual charged 33-tick drain follows. Drain is not voluntary rest and can mute terminal differences. |
| Persistence | One world within a bounded 1–1024-tick run; no snapshot-resume or wall-clock daemon. |
| Storage | Completed scenes/events/boundaries grow; bounded live work does not imply bounded observer memory. |
| Build | Frozen-source module-path host and extracted bootstrap add coupling/cold compilation; Windows ASCII workspace tested. |
| Acceptance | Independent 023 review and a separate protection decision remain; no default adoption follows. |

The prospective architecture's estimate of 32 episodes was incorrect. Actual
inherited capacity 16 is bound in the pre-run source freeze and reached but never
exceeded in original histories. Capacity 32 applies to evidence/provenance/learning
cells. A supplemental regression enforces 16 in both modes. Original runtime,
specification and archives were preserved. Trust, expectations and concerns may
legitimately survive episode eviction under their established contracts.

## Material exhaustion is not the only problem

In long pair 18/19, Continuous performs 172 scenes and 87 transfers but consumes
only three of five initial units. Two owned units remain while everyone's hunger
reaches 100. Transfers circulate food; they create none. In scarcity pair 22/23,
all initial food really is consumed and contact continues without supply. Both
matter: finite sources end, and existing values can neglect already owned food.

Clean pair 10/11 isolates a narrower cost: identical actor-local state at tick 7
except public budget; Invite 64 beats Consume 5, delaying the meal one tick before
a Leave/no-transfer encounter. Actor 2's hunger area rises five. In 4/5 all four
areas are higher under Continuous. Other fixtures show mixed or lower individual
integrals. These are heuristic-specific consequences, not a total welfare ranking.

## Decision on finite-input preparation

Continuous scheduling is mechanically sufficient to support a separately reviewed
material-process study. The next material candidate remains narrow finite-input
preparation, likely 024 only after independent 023 review, any separately accepted
integrity pass, and new authorization/preregistration. The immediate next action is
that review, including the valuation/consumption failure. Conversion must not be
introduced merely to rescue these histories or make them busier.

| Proposed element | Requirement before implementation |
|---|---|
| Finite raw stock | Explicit source, units and initial total; no spontaneous food or replenishment timer. |
| Acquisition | Bounded owned observation, elapsed cost, contention, depleted/failed claims and independent choice. |
| Preparation | Actual reserved time crossing reports, competing with social/meal/inquiry opportunities. |
| In-process material | Explicit owned escrow and bounded active batches; unfinished-work/cancellation law. |
| Finished food | Existing Offer/Accept/Refuse/Consume accounting only after actual completion. |
| Waste | Persistent ledger; illustrative two raw → one edible plus one waste needs separate registration. |
| Conservation | Source + owned raw + in-process + finished + waste + consumed, at every physical change. |
| Choice | Pure, local, replaceable heuristic without future usefulness, truth, private inventory or a general planner. |
| Opportunity cost | Preserve non-use, late work, waste, missed meetings, failed acquisition and worse hunger. |
| Validation | Immutable originals, independent primitive reader, exact replay and corruption controls. |

Do not change contact scores in the same intervention without an independently
registered comparison. Otherwise new supply, valuation and scheduling are confounded.
A legitimate preparation opportunity may be ignored or fail to cause consumption;
that is evidence to preserve. Finite conversion does not establish renewable or
indefinite survival. Farms, jobs, money, markets, settlements and institutions
remain outside this authorization.

## Before calling this a minimum viable community

Require reviewed reproducible continuity; meaningful locally chosen material
acquisition/conversion/use; stock/escrow/waste/consumed conservation; bounded owned
information preserving mistakes and forgetting contracts; independent consent under
resource/time competition; consequences beyond a single exchange; adverse/null
conditions and source/order/phase/cost sensitivity without retuning; practical
live-versus-audit cost; and an explicit independently reviewed definition of viability.
Four busy actors and more exchanges alone do not meet that standard.

## Cost and open question

The 42 originals occupy 75.344 MB decoded and 1.811 MB gzip. Peak recording-process
working set 13.537 MB includes observer audit; it is not deep live-cognition heap.
Live commitments/pending tokens peak at four; actual episodic capacity is 16. New
report records hold only IDs/references. At 768 ordinary ticks Continuous records
18.566 MB, with median simulation 52.747 ms / serialization 34.084 ms over three
local samples. Windowed records 31.838 MB and 34.267 / 52.177 ms. Worlds differ,
so no general speed ranking follows. Replay and independent-reader costs are
separate. Source-preserving compilation has substantial cold setup cost. No
optimization pass, population scaling, consciousness, natural social emergence or
sustainable community life is established.

**Strongest open question:** when continuous permission removes artificial quiet
feeding windows, how should bounded local values trade off contact, consumption
and later material work? This experiment exposes that question without answering
it through a new planner or invented supply.

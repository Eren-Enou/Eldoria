competition, executed cooperation and persistent resource histories while preserving
conservation, locality and shared time? The original specification, gate, source,
complete histories, comparisons, longitudinal measurements and causal slices were

## Physical conservation, identity and time

One stable CacheId0 owns finite nonnegative stock. Every reconstructed operation
preserves `initial owned + initial cache = current owned + current cache + meals`.
Transfers change ownership; meals destroy one unit. Grants atomically debit cache
stock and checked-add one owned unit. Depleted/overflow resolution changes neither.
Overflow is unit-tested but does not occur in the registered matrix. Capacity is
not a production process; Owned's nominal capacity can exceed its post-placement
stock0, an already disclosed specification/metadata qualification.

All free actors decide before any new reservation. A claim reserves its actor, not
a unit. Completion rechecks actual stock. Due claims sort by deadline and declared
Ascending, Descending or Rotating rank using the admission wave. Legal deterministic
ordering is not fairness. All registered claims end exactly after2/4/8 ticks; a
loser pays the same full time with no refund or compensating fallback. At most three
claims are live. A private020 wrapper prevents unrelated generic Simulation calls
while claims exist. Completion releases only its own actor.

The recurring clock advances all hunger once per elapsed tick (+5 per4, cap100).
Resource actions precede claim completion, which precedes already-selected free
actors' meals within a tick. Concurrent work does not charge duplicate world time.
No claimant appears in a resource pair, another selected activity, meal or recipient
response while busy. A two-tick completion cannot retroactively supply consent
that was unavailable at the earlier response phase. This is covered by regression
and original reconstruction, not inferred solely from endpoint balance equality.

## Local choice and matched controls

Policy sees own Agent/profile/bounded inquiry projection, public availability,
current cache ID/stock/access/time/duration and public inquiry-lane availability.
It receives no scenario, seed, rival hunger/inventory/traits, future winner, hidden
provenance, actual future response or observer history. Fresh observations are an
explicit co-present sensing channel. The private-helpful/cautious mirror preserves
the first focal input/choice despite changed hidden recipient circumstances.

Need appends Acquire scored at own hunger after Wait/Consume/Invite, using strict
first maximum. Acquire prioritizes eligible acquisition, otherwise the old ranking.
Eligibility requires free actor, food0, hunger>0, observed positive accessible stock
and enough remaining time. Ignored records the option but retains old selection.
Mixed score units and empty-inventory priority are heuristics, not utility estimates.

Closed/Ignored/Need/Acquire share initial owned food, cache quantity, profiles,
prepared cognition, horizon and opportunities. Access differs for Closed; policy
differs for the open modes. All five controls have equal potential supply. Owned
instead distributes cache units cyclically into initial inventories: it changes
placement and immediate availability, not merely acquisition cost. Access can
therefore explain gains against Closed without creating additional material supply.
Post-choice divergence is carried forward; no reset restores matched trajectories.

## Inquiry, architecture and performance qualifications

All22 executed inquiries are Direct;11 acquire receipts and change support/status,
11 do neither. Evidence's33-tick bound cannot fit24/4-tick windows. This is an
admission restriction, not evidence of superior method choice. Concern age, wave,
availability, hunger and later social feedback co-vary; cognitive changes are not
isolated effects of acquisition knowledge.

Pending claims mask inquiry candidates. New same-cohort claims can reject an
otherwise provisionally executable atomic query, with an explicit record and no
fallback. The registered matrix never exercises that second branch. Original
reader code checks it if present, but absence of a witness is not empirical branch
coverage. No generic concurrent query scheduler or interior query choices exist.
A future prospective boundary test is justified before claiming broader concurrency.

The isolated562-line lifecycle duplicates recurring consumption, preparation,
completion and handshake bookkeeping. This protects old sources but creates drift
risk; the private wrapper and exact old control narrow exposure. Generalizing
population/scheduling must not silently copy this three-person assumption. Live
physical state is small and claims bounded; observer histories grow unbounded.

Original engineering measurements:606,846,590 raw trace bytes,11,207,457 compressed;
schema-only deduplication reduced raw audit size35.8% with reversed-encoding equality.
One50-run ongoing measurement reports Owned about2.17×019 Baseline execution time
despite matching behavior; serialized size grows7.7%. Need's33.7% size increase also
contains changed behavior. These are single-machine engineering observations,
not controlled benchmarks or scientific replications. No benchmark is needed for
this read-only documentation/verification addition.

## Accepted and unsupported claims

Demonstrated A–J: finite physical source, legitimate current perception, voluntary
selection, success, depletion failure, spent time, changed owned inventory,
subsequent consumption/cooperation, displaced others' opportunities and persistent
recorded consequences. The universal inert-effects explanation fails in these
fixtures. Uniform benefit and monotonic gains from faster acquisition also fail.

Mechanism-dependent: stock visibility, one cache, three stationary actors, fixed
windows, initial trait extremes, score scales, arbitration and meal alignment.
Seed/order/duration variations are parameter sensitivity, not statistical evidence.
Ambiguous: prevalence outside constructed fixtures and causal shares of later
trust/information differences among their multiple mediators.

Unsupported K/L: generally improved resource allocation, optimal foraging, calibrated
opportunity cost, sustainable economy, autonomous community, human motivation or
social emergence. No persistent environmental knowledge exists: current known-zero
exclusion is structural, not sophisticated source learning. Eviction never implies
erasure of separately persistent effects; observer history never supplies cognition.

The strongest next question is whether bounded legitimately acquired environmental
observations can remain causally useful or costly when other actors change physical
stock without updating one's knowledge. Investigating that requires prospective
observation/retention controls, not hiding stock retrospectively in020 archives.

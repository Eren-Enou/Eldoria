## Question, intervention and registration

Can the same four individuals continue legitimate independent work across reports,
with local knowledge, independent consent, shared physical time and conservation?
The 42 registered worlds comprise 12 controlled pairs, eight sensitivity pairs and
two reporting placebos. All use seed 42; these are deterministic fixture/parameter
comparisons, not independent population observations.

Windowed exposes `start + (floor((now-start)/period)+1)*period`. Continuous exposes
`now+33`. That projection is recomputed after atomic inquiries. Reporting annotates
history; it does not reset actors or close existing work. New ordinary choices stop
at nominal T. Already caused protocol/work completes with actual charges and need
progression; both worlds end at T+33. Internal resource actions in previously started
work can execute during drain. This is bounded evaluation, not an unbounded service.

The 33 bound is inherited Evidence cost 28 plus protocol/follow-up allowance five.
It is not fitted to successful outcomes. Primary period 11 cannot fit even a Direct
reservation of 15, so it is a deliberately severe control. Period 48 sensitivity
cases permit inquiries in both modes. Reporting placebos vary period 11→17 under
Continuous and preserve exact underlying histories. Epoch and Absolute phase are
prospectively explicit. This does not repair or erase 022's phase discrepancy.

## Clock, legality and cost limitations

One Runtime clock advances disjoint commitments; no sum-of-scene time duplication,
double booking or phantom transfer was found. Actual need/event/claim/meal order
reconstructs all physical captures and final states. Source conservation holds at
each operation. Nominal cutoffs, protocol drain, atomic skips, reporting interior
and true release are distinct. More exchanges, retained food or lower hunger area
are separate dimensions rather than a comprehensive social utility.

Original storage is 75,343,581 decoded / 1,811,033 gzip bytes. Reported process peak
includes observer audit; it is not deep live-cognition heap. Existing measurements
are local engineering samples, not evidence of scalability. Four co-present actors,
controlled bootstrap, fixed sensing, exclusive queries, finite evaluation/drain,
capped hunger, growing observer history and source-host compilation coupling limit
interpretation. No new runtime benchmark is warranted by this read-only review.

## Accepted and unsupported conclusions

Demonstrated: public opportunity projection changes voluntary ranking/eligibility,
actual execution and some persistent physical/social consequences; work crosses
reports under one conserved clock; reporting alone need not change behavior.
Harmful, null, mixed and reconvergent cases remain.

Mechanism-dependent: invitation dominance, delayed meals and retained food follow
the inherited independently scaled heuristic values and constructed opportunities.
Ambiguous: net value of relationship changes, unique contribution of one transferred
unit, and longer-term benefit beyond measured bounds.
Unsupported: global superiority, calibrated utility, optimal agency, natural social
emergence, psychological realism, sustainable community, indefinite live service
or automatic adoption. These conclusions, source identities, negative/null witnesses
and exact originals are suitable for a separate qualified 001–023 integrity pass.

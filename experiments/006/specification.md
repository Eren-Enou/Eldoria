# Experiment 006 — adaptive inquiry hypothesis (before implementation)

2026-10-07. Repository and Experiments 001–005 are the baseline; no dependencies,
old rules or archives will be replaced. The existing 005 model predicts answers,
penalizes failure and sometimes abandons concerns. This opt-in extension instead
learns usefulness for a concern × source × strategy and permits stopping a method
while leaving the concern open.

Minimal strategies: Direct question, Evidence request, Pause. Direct and Evidence
reuse voluntary local communication policies and event-specific receipt/revision
accounting. No natural language, hidden-state search or planner. Alternative sources
are actual co-present known individuals, not global experts; an unfamiliar person
can legitimately have no knowledge and answer with silence. The experiment should
report that exploratory source switching can fail rather than invent expertise.

Inquiry input contains own concerns, hunger/relationship context, directed local
credibility, bounded received information and source-strategy estimates, plus public
co-presence and advertised evidence opportunities. It excludes partner state,
sensor outcomes/channel/seed, objective truth and deceptive intent. Availability
announcements themselves are voluntary, scored from the source's own local input.
Configuring source evidence capabilities is an audited experimental intervention,
not a scheduled message. Receipt/source identity is stable; relabeling repeated
evidence must not manufacture new information.

Novelty categories: New, Stronger, Conflict, Redundant, None. Information signatures
retain the event, real source/provenance, claim and quality. Same weak repeated
content has zero realized value; a stronger observation about the same claim has
positive value. Conflicting information is useful for recognizing uncertainty,
not automatically certainty. Bound per-agent estimates/signatures/episodes and
audit eviction. Preserve stable receipt and concern IDs in the archive.

Initial useful-value priors favor cheaper direct questions; update only selected
strategy/source from actual voluntary responses. Estimate value separately from
concern importance. Compare expected benefit (importance × estimated value) with
explicit inquiry effort; Pause wins ties and leaves status active. A public new
evidence opportunity can raise estimated value only when its provenance/quality
could add information beyond the actor's retained receipts. Repeated identical
announcements cannot repeatedly revive a depleted method.

Controlled tests A–F: weak answer repeated; history-driven strategy change; pause
then renewed evidence opportunity; locally met alternative source; high importance
with no useful answer; useful repetition of Evidence after stronger evidence becomes
accessible. Compare identical recorded input with learning/novelty ablated, preserving
every other score input. Do not force queries or responses to meet a story.

Evaluation: 128 seeded suites with full replay, causal candidate/response/novelty
traces, old archive compatibility under 006, 100/1,000-agent matched 005/006 benchmarks,
serialized audit growth and selected method/source changes/pauses/unresolved matters.
No heap or large-scale claim without measurement. Full audit remains unbounded.

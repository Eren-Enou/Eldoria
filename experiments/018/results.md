repeated voluntary choices and can change executed contacts,transfers,relationships
and later material circumstances. Existing local mechanisms suffice for this narrow
capability;no new planner or cognitive cells were needed. Extra opportunities to eat
while others negotiate often change only timing or intermediate state,and sometimes
leave people hungrier. Finite stocks,short admission horizons,mixed activity scores
and ordering strongly limit interpretation. No default adoption or018 foundation
protection. An independent018 scientific review remains future work. No019 begun.

## Question and minimum architecture

Can recurring needs and independent choices among eating,resource interaction,inquiry
and waiting repeatedly affect other individuals' opportunities and persistent history?
Separate mechanical pressure,eating/activity selection,current availability,actual
interaction,learning/relationship change,and material consequences carried forward.
Different scores or observer records alone do not demonstrate executed social effects.

Reuse017's three-person resource commitments,old resource policy/resolver,005 concerns,
006/007 inquiry,008 Grouped,009 FIFO and012 Contextual. Every available actor chooses
from its own state;recipients independently consent or decline under common Continue0.
No016 retry cells. One resource commitment,three current invitations,zero new cognitive
cells. The base ECS can progress disjoint resource scenes,but experimental contact
projection/handshake and atomic-query interleaving require additional prospective
work for four people. Four-person concurrency was excluded to isolate recurrence;
this study does not test whether it would make effects more robust.

The new physical clock stores epoch,last synchronized tick and settings. For a→b,
pulses=floor((b−epoch)/I)−floor((a−epoch)/I),then H=min(100,H+5*pulses).
Default I4;sensitivities2/16;Fixed controls increment0. Consumption removes one owned
food and reduces current hunger by40,floor0,after that tick's progression. No food
production,reset,metabolic realism,health,death or hidden activity-specific penalty.
All actors accumulate pressure through the same shared time,including during inquiry,
waiting and occupation. Batch charges equal repeated increments without intervening
material actions. Policy evaluation count does not determine hunger.

Old resource events and free-person consumption share one global tick. Send/response
cost two;resource reservation8,Direct15,Evidence33 include handshake. Actual resolver
time is charged. Busy actors cannot choose incompatible activities;actual termination
releases them. Inquiry remains atomic to the third person,but its internal need
progression applies to everyone. All-Wait charges one tick in every018 mode;periods
carry state with no mandatory meal or extra three meal ticks. This common prospective
cadence/meal change is not attributed to the need factor or retrofitted onto017.

## Scientific classification and remaining question

Demonstrated:common elapsed-time recurrence across actual activities;repeated voluntary
self-maintenance/contact/inquiry decisions;real occupation/current availability;changed
executed resource responses/transfers and persistent need/relationship consequences.
Complete and partial nulls,and harmful extra opportunity,are also demonstrated.
Existing mechanisms suffice for these narrow effects;minimum mechanism necessity is
not identified. The experiment does not demonstrate generally good allocation.

Mechanism-dependent:the5/I cap100 law,one-unit40-pressure meal,H versus Invite score
comparison,Continue0,conservative8/15/33 bounds,one-tick idle cohorts,external period
closure,atomic inquiries,three people,stable IDs and finite initial stocks. Ambiguous:
isolated mediation of terminal outcomes,ecological prevalence,optimal consumption
timing,need-rate calibration,minimum activity set,and whether more partners or truly
disjoint activities would change the finding. Repeated cycles alone are not emergence.
The common synchronized need phase is another external modeling assumption;this
study does not test heterogeneous individual need rhythms.

Unsupported:realistic metabolism,human-like motivation,calibrated utility,optimal
welfare,fair matching,friendship,language,institutions,culture,sustainable economies,
consciousness,intelligence or autonomous civilization. No research mechanism becomes
a default;018 is not added to001–017 protection. No019 is started.

Strongest open question:do these opportunity-mediated effects survive less artificial
horizon/decision timing and a defensible local valuation of consumption,or are most
costly choices consequences of the inherited mixed score scales and finite stock?
The present evidence does not justify resolving that question by retuning018 afterward.

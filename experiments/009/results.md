# Experiment 009: bounded selective retention

Completed 2026-10-08, `experiment-009-v1`, baseline `e05bfaa`.

The experiment demonstrates a narrow retention benefit at the unchanged 32-item
assessment capacity. It does **not** justify replacing FIFO as the default. Quality
and Salient preserve conflict and older evidence under redundant traffic; Salient
additionally protects a weak unresolved concern in one controlled and one held-out
combination. Both selective methods make predictable mistakes. There is no positive
sharing-action advantage in these fixtures, and FIFO makes the better action in the
controlled minor-later-useful case. The opt-in alternatives remain research modes.

## Rule and causal boundary

[Specification](specification.md) records A (FIFO sufficient), B (local salience
helps), and C (privileged information explains gains), before implementation. Its
closing review notes disclose subsequent harness corrections. Do not characterize
this as a blind, untouched preregistration or independent external test set.

All modes retain finalized008 Grouped aggregation and the same 32-item cap. FIFO
removes the oldest item. Quality assigns received quality to one representative of
each retained local event/origin/sign, zero to duplicate copies. Salient adds current
active own concern importance to that representative. Highest quality, newest equal
quality chooses the representative; minimum score, oldest arrival chooses eviction.
Incoming items can be rejected. Opposing signs remain separate representatives.

Policy inputs contain only own retained candidates plus the incoming receipt and
own capped-eight concerns. No scenario name, truth, future query, hidden parent/root,
sensor configuration, partner state or observer history reaches the policy. Public
Known origin tokens are received attribution, not secretly inferred lineage. New
local fields are no evidence summary. Scores are recomputed at each receipt.

Attribution modifies matching **retained** Unknown assumptions before scoring. It
cannot reconstruct evicted contents. Later delivery is a new receipt, even if it
legitimately refers to the same still-available provider acquisition. A zero-quality
new acquired report contains an explicit Boolean claim with zero received weight.
It requests reappraisal of the remaining basis without restoring an old acquisition.

Evicting unrelated-event assessment evidence does not rewrite all cached beliefs,
episodes, concerns, trust, credibility or inquiry estimates. Their previously
established lifetimes still apply. The cached80 belief in FIFO's flood persists
until the actual zero-quality target receipt then becomes0; this is no archive
recovery. The episode and relationship consequences are separate from the basis.
The finalized001–008 contract map and all historical evidence remain unchanged.

## Controlled results

Each condition uses nine agents, six actual scarcity refusals, matched profile and
resource interventions, the same delivery sequence and the same opportunities.
Points are probes of the existing local resource policy at matched present conditions,
not executed food transfers or new narrative events. Native delivery fixtures and
explicit acquisition opportunities isolate retention; they do not measure naturally
chosen inquiry frequency. Full snapshots include trust, credibility, inquiry history,
concern transitions, resource accounts and immutable provenance records.

Numbers below are final retained-basis support after legitimate reappraisal. See
[readable traces](report.txt), [summary](summary.json), and [comparisons](comparisons.json).

Before counterevidence, the wrong item remains-80 for selective versus 0 for FIFO.
The original refusals actually involved scarce donor food 1/hunger 90. That observer

later correct evidence, selective's10 is uncertain rather than an opposing belief.

truth accuracy. Known repetition still occupies physical receipt slots: this rule
scores duplicates down; it does not compress many receipts into one hidden summary.

The six original refusals saturate trust at-100. Preserving one target's evidence
usually cannot overcome the other five consequences, so most sharing probes remain

to force a selective win. Only the minor-later-useful controlled comparison changes
final action between FIFO and Salient, and favors FIFO. Redelivery produces an Offer
in all controlled modes. No scenario forces the policy's action.

## Held-out sensitivity and falsification

Four combinations vary quality 20/45/70/90, actual goal 0/40/60/100, distinct/repeated
traffic quality, known/hidden attribution, reversed/interleaved topic order, shifted
event identities and early/late arrival of the minor item. These reuse scenario
families, so they test parameter sensitivity rather than independent task transfer.
Each variant has all three modes. Initial base snapshots and every incoming item
including received quality, attribution and communicator match across the methods;
subsequent concern changes are causal outcomes rather than secretly reset controls.

Across all four combinations, both selective methods retain consequential evidence
and both conflict signs that FIFO loses. Final flood support is respectively
20/45/70/90 for selective versus 0 for FIFO. Explicit attribution changes hidden-case
support from 75 to 50 in variants 1/3, consistent with the received origins. A hidden
shared origin can still receive inappropriate confidence; the rule is no lineage
truth detector. Revelation never restores previously evicted acquisitions.

Salient uniquely protects weak 45 at goal 40 against distinct 60 traffic in variant 1;
Quality and FIFO retain no target basis. At goal 0/quality 20 there is no retained
concern bonus, so all lose it. At quality 70 the concern already resolves and all
lose that item against80 traffic. At quality 90 both selective methods retain it
without the bonus. The extra concern signal distinguishes exactly one of twelve
controlled groups and one of 48 held-out groups, not a broad superiority claim.

The expiry prediction fails for variant 3: quality 90 remains stronger than distinct 70
even after resolution, so selective retains90 while FIFO forgets. Removing concern
bonus is not general decay or a guarantee of practical forgetting. Important evidence
can nevertheless be forgotten in the equal-value and resolved-weaker conditions.

FIFO retains a better later-useful basis in all four minor cases:50 for early arrival,
60 for late, versus 0 for selective. All held-out final actions are Leave, including
these cases: shifting the history adds a seventh original refusal whose persistent
negative contribution remains. This changes the behavioral threshold but is matched
within each comparison. There are **zero** held-out action differences. Do not
conflate preserved evidence with demonstrated improved enacted behavior.

All180 scenario/configuration/method outcome groups have128 identical final outcomes
across seeds0–127:4,608 controlled and 18,432 held-out trials, each rerun exactly.
This repetition establishes determinism, not180 independent statistical observations
or a calibrated memory model. Extreme-seed tests also replay the held-out suite.

A is too strong if read as equivalence of retained distinctions: FIFO loses conflict
and weak-concern evidence in distinguishing cases. B receives narrow support for
local retained distinctions, but broad improvement of later behavior is unproven.
The universal-win reading of B is falsified by wrong-item retention, minor-item loss
and expiry failure. C is not needed for these gains: the structural input boundary
and matched receipt tests exclude the proposed privileged signals. This is not a
proof about every arbitrary replacement function a caller could write.

Quality explains most gains. The concern bonus has a specific held-out benefit, so
it is not equivalent on every distinction, but its practical advantage remains small.
Keep FIFO as the default; do not promote Salient to a universal contract. No extra
recency weights, future-value labels, global summaries, contradiction bonuses or
scenario-specific rules were added. Those alternatives are untested, not empirically
falsified. The chosen unit concern weight remains heuristic, not optimized psychology.

## Capacity, storage and performance

Live assessment remains32 items per individual; concerns remain8; all other mode
capacities remain unchanged. There is no new per-individual score cache or summary.
The simulation adds one mode, one prospective assessment-start cursor, a function
pointer and an observer record vector:40 bytes for the Retention resource payload
plus an8-byte function pointer on this host, excluding ECS headers/allocations.
Each Item and Concern payload is72 bytes; each Candidate is32 bytes. A decision transiently holds at most 33 items,
33 candidate metadata records and 8 cloned concerns **per Input**. Runtime validation
and immutable capture use multiple transient copies: up to 32 retained items plus
33 input items plus 33 decision items, and two sets of 8 concerns, before temporary
buffers are dropped in the normal built-in path (9,264 bytes of these payloads,
excluding vector headers, allocations and support maps). The invalid-output fallback
uses additional bounded copies of the same 33/8 Input. These copies contain the same retained/arriving information,
not additional logical acquisitions; their physical cost still counts. See
[layout payload sizes](layout.json); allocations/ECS headers and RSS are not measured.
Representation uniqueness costs
at most 33² comparisons, concern relevance at most 33×8, and selection is linear.
Grouped assessment is unchanged; recording support for all retained event IDs adds
up to 32 Grouped computations per before/after map. Audit replay walks receipt history
once with the bounded local basis, rather than rescoring from the current policy.

Full inputs, candidate scores, eviction and before/after supports are append-only
observer audit, with growth O(receipts × (33 + 8 + event-support entries)). They are
never consulted to repopulate cognition. This is substantial experimental overhead,
not a production storage solution. Only new 009 archives are compressed, with zero
gzip timestamp; compression does not expand cognitive capacity or delete evidence.
Controlled JSON 49,468,354 bytes compresses to 1,053,254; held-out206,624,726 to 4,275,623.
[Manifest](archive-manifest.json) records hashes and sizes of both forms.

uses historical captured choices; tests independently recompute built-in policy

capacity/ties/attribution and preserve the frozen008 FIFO validator. The direct
native invalid-policy path rejects before cognition or clock changes. Inside an
already-paid automatic or voluntary protocol, invalid replacement output instead
uses locally computed FIFO and records the validation reason with the actual
fallback decision. A separate regression verifies provenance delivery still completes
and audits its time/receipt without panicking or losing a protocol record. Built-in
methods never require fallback; this changes no earlier-mode valid outputs.

hook and selected-slot internal assessment adapter; shared immutable receipt checks
were extracted from 008 without changing its valid outputs. Tests are
`tests/experiment009.rs`; evaluator is `examples/evaluate009.rs`. All evidence is
under 009. The cross-experiment 001–008 contract stays frozen; a future separate

The strongest unresolved question is whether the narrow retained-distinction benefit
changes voluntarily chosen inquiry or executed resource outcomes across multiple
competing concerns and longer histories, enough to justify its audit/runtime cost.
This experiment does not establish that, human-like memory, general planning,
statistical evidence independence, consciousness or broad social emergence.

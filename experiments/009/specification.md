Question: at exactly32 assessment items, can retention using locally available
salience preserve behaviorally useful distinctions better than FIFO, without truth,
hidden lineage, future-query knowledge or an auxiliary evidence summary?

Competing explanations: A FIFO is sufficient and extra complexity is unjustified;
B local salience can protect useful distinctions under pressure; C apparent gains
depend on privileged information and must be rejected. B is not assumed.

## Candidate and simpler control

Freeze008 Grouped assessment, received quality, origin identity, belief/revision/
trust/concern/inquiry mechanisms and all cognitive capacities.009 is prospective
and opt-in after Grouped assessment. Three replaceable eviction policies:

- FIFO: oldest retained item at overflow, exactly008's control.
- Quality: preserve one representative of each locally known event/origin/sign at
  received quality; redundant copies score0. Representative is highest quality,
  newest arrival on equal quality. Unique representatives score received quality.
- Salient: same redundancy and quality rule, plus importance of a currently active
  retained own concern for that item's event. Only representatives get this bonus.

At overflow evaluate existing32 plus incoming item, after legitimate attribution
updates only retained matching assumptions. Evict minimum score; equal scores evict
oldest arrival. Incoming may itself be rejected. Below capacity retain all deliveries.
Do not sum duplicate counts or reward frequency. A different Unknown speaker is a
locally distinct origin even if an observer knows it shares a root. Opposing signs
are separate representatives so contradiction is not erased merely by deduplication.
These integer ordering rules are heuristics, not calibrated psychology.

Allowed inputs: own currently retained assessment items, incoming received item,
and own bounded concerns (event/status/importance); received quality, publicly known
origin and arrival order. No truth, correctness, hidden roots/parents/depth, partner
state, sensor seed/channel/outcome before receipt, observer archives, scenario label,
future inquiry/action/query, or future concern state. No stored salience history.
All scoring is recomputed; transient input holds at most33 candidates and8 concerns.
New live metadata is the selected method/function and prospective assessment-start cursor only. Full captured decision input
is append-only observer audit, never read by policy/resolver for retention.

## Controlled matrix (predictions, not required outcomes)

Every matter begins with an actual policy/resolver refusal. Information enters
existing validated native or voluntary provenance channels. Physical/profile/sensor
interventions are recorded; no memories or roots are inserted. Direct delivery
fixtures isolate retention, not prevalence of naturally chosen inquiry.

| Case | Distinction and prediction |
|---|---|
| Consequential flood | Old strong event vs34 low-quality repeated receipts on unrelated event; selective may retain old basis while FIFO loses it |
| Unresolved concern | Old weak active concern vs repeated unrelated evidence; Salient may outperform Quality as well as FIFO |
| Relevance expires | Old concern legitimately resolves, then enough distinct stronger new evidence exceeds32; old basis should be evictable |
| Conflict | Two opposing strong origins then redundant flood; both signs may survive selective retention and preserve uncertainty |
| Known repetition | Same known acquired origin is repeatedly delivered; repetition cannot raise any representative's score |
| Hidden shared origin | Two speakers legitimately deliver unattributed shared evidence; local attention may regard them as distinct |
| Later attribution | Reveal one matching hidden origin after pressure; update retained assumptions only, never restore earlier evicted contents |
| Equal salience | More than32 equal-valued representatives; oldest deterministic tie loses |
| Salient wrong | High-quality/important but inverted evidence is retained; wrong confidence remains possible |
| Minor later useful | Low-importance, low-quality earlier item followed by stronger traffic, later legitimate relevance; selective can lose what FIFO retained |
| Event traffic | Vary which unrelated event produces traffic, preserving target basis/content; test event-sensitive retention and busy-topic crowding |
| Redelivery | Forgotten target is voluntarily delivered again; new receipt, not archive reconstruction |

At least one intentionally unfavorable control: low-value incoming/new item rejected
by selective in a full high-value basis, retained by FIFO; later local probe concerns
that item. This exposes inability to foresee the later opportunity. Resolved high-
quality evidence may occupy attention after its practical relevance falls; report it.

Direct comparisons share seed, initial world, all delivered content/quality/known
attribution/speakers, concern initialization, capacity, sequence and opportunities;
only retention differs. Concern consequences can subsequently diverge causally and
are measured, not reset secretly. Record local concern state at every decision and
distinguish fixed-context pure-policy controls from full trajectory comparisons.

## Evaluation and falsification

Primary measures: retained target origins/signs; persistence of conflict; unresolved-
concern basis; repeated-displacement resistance; final local support and matched-
present action after an actual legitimate reappraisal receipt. Measure these apart
from observer correctness. A zero-quality new reading may request reappraisal but
conveys no forgotten content. Cached beliefs/trust retain their008 lifetimes; do not
pretend unrelated-event eviction automatically updates every other belief.

Record every incoming item, local input/candidates/scores/eviction, basis before/after,
all affected event support, concern/status/importance, trust/credibility/inquiry where
relevant, received references and matched-present probes. Preserve immutable causal
records even for dropped items. Exact replay seeds0–127 plus extreme seeds in tests.

Held-out plan, frozen after rule choice and before evaluation: vary quality in
{20,45,70,90}, importance via actual own profile interventions, both conflict signs,
known vs hidden attribution, recency, shifted event IDs, multiple traffic topics,
interleaved/reversed arrival order and whether future-relevant item arrives early
or late. Report all variations, including worse/ambiguous outcomes; do not tune scores
or control parameters after observing wins. Compare Quality to Salient: if it achieves
equivalent distinctions, retain the simpler candidate or reject extra concern scoring.

Reject claimed advantage if it requires privileged inputs, unlimited summaries,
disguised capacity, scenario labels, inability to forget/make mistakes, no meaningful
held-out advantage, loss under changed traffic/order, or equivalent simpler rule.
Distinguish universal robustness (can fail) from specific controlled benefits.
Negative results count as a completed experiment; do not erase failed predictions.

## Harness review before full evaluation

The seed42 preview exposed saturation: six original refusals leave trust at -100,
so preserved target evidence often changes assessment without changing the sharing
probe. Keep that setup and report the limitation; do not retune it to force action
wins. Before executing held-out evaluation, replace hardcoded weak/distinct quality
in the relevant fixtures with recorded configuration fields: default40/70 remains
unchanged; held-out weak quality20/45/70/90 and distinct quality90/60/80/70.
Alternate explicit attribution in held-out hidden/revelation cases. This corrects
parameter coverage, not the score rule or controlled outcomes. Positive-quality
origin counts exclude the zero-quality reappraisal origin. Initial fixture cases
are designed, not blind preregistered external data; held-out combinations test
parameter sensitivity, not independent real-world generalization.

Scenario completeness review adds repeated delivery of the same known acquisition
after the unresolved-concern distinct-capacity pressure, and a later legitimate
90-quality opposing native reading after the salient-wrong checkpoint. These close
the required redundancy and later-error controls; they do not change scores or
existing pressure sequences. The wrong-item retention measure uses its pre-counter-
evidence checkpoint; final assessment also reports legitimate conflicting evidence.

Held-out minor-item recency uses an explicit `minor_early` field: true for variants
0/2, false for1/3; the controlled incoming-late fixture stays unchanged. A required
plan detail was missing in the initial harness. All harness corrections are disclosed
here after the preview rather than claimed as an untouched blind preregistration.

Replacement-output error handling: explicit native `communicate` rejects invalid
retention output before receipt/clock mutation. Nested automatic or voluntary
protocols may already have paid time/contact costs; they complete the legitimate
receipt with locally computed FIFO, and record the bounded validation reason beside
the actual fallback decision. This preserves their existing accounting/termination
without accepting invalid scores or pretending to roll back earlier actions. Built-in
methods do not trigger fallback. No new candidate weighting or capacity is involved.

The zero-quality reappraisal report contains a legitimately received Boolean claim;
its zero weight adds no positive-quality support. Earlier "no new content" wording
means no old acquisition is reconstructed, not that the receipt lacks any content.
All such receipts and origins are explicitly retained in the causal trace.

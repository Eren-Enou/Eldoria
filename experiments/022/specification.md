# Experiment 022: Four-Individual Concurrent Activities and Shared-World Scheduling

Prospective registration at gate D/E, before runtime implementation or outcomes.
No default adoption, no extension of the 001–021 foundation, no Experiment 023.

Question: can four independent individuals conduct two disjoint social encounters
and legitimate environmental work under one clock with local knowledge, consent,
conservation, deterministic arbitration and persistent history?

Nulls: local choices never yield two disjoint accepted contacts; capacity changes
availability but not material/social outcomes; overlap merely saves time without
benefit; finite food and stale information dominate any concurrency benefit.
Harmful and mixed outcomes are valid. More transfers or contacts are not welfare.

Mechanisms and exact phases follow
[`concurrent-world-architecture.md`](../../docs/development/concurrent-world-architecture.md).
Use old scarcity resource policy, invitation value, Continue-zero recipient,
006/007 query, Grouped008/FIFO009/Contextual012, Need priority and LastSeen021.
Do not retune scores. Four authoritative actors IDs0–3 retain all old cognitive
caps. New live registry max4, pending proposals max4, environmental slots4x1.
One resource scene remains bounded at six turns; claims use duration2/4/8;
send/reply/meal each cost one tick. Query remains explicitly exclusive.

The only primary intervention is social capacity2 versus1. Initial world,
bootstrap history, profiles, cache, cadence, need law, horizon and public
opportunities match. Do not reset after divergence. All other people remain
possible partners. Prior helpful/refusal episodes, when used, are real executed
bootstrap scenes; pre-period circumstances are declared interventions. They do
not script later invitations or consent. Store bootstrap events and initial state.

## Measurement and falsification

Record the whole path: owned state → candidate inputs/scores → invitation →
recipient opportunity/response → admission → commitments → actual events and
physical outcomes → release → later choice/state. Measure overlapping intervals,
same/different completion times, lost contacts, failures, meals, material ledger,
semantic trust, hunger area and final owned state. A missing intended witness
is a limitation, never a reason to tune scores or force actions.

Reject concurrency evidence if overlapping actors are double booked, scenes
advance the clock separately, deadlines are skipped, consent is fabricated,
unobserved stock/private state/future enters selection, archive restores memory,
completion releases another scene, IDs collide, accounting fails, matching fails,
or independent reconstruction cannot support the claimed result.

## Reproduction and gates

record source/policy locks and compare exact replay. A standard-library independent
reader must reconstruct clock, reservations, responses, core arithmetic, physical
operations, local memories, ledger and final state; scratch corruption controls
cover participant/ID/deadline/order/consent/ownership/final-state failures.

normal foundation and standalones, 022 evaluator/replay/reader and throughput.

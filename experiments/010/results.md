# Experiment 010: survival-to-action

Completed 2026-10-08, baseline `cf82e30`. Retained differences can change voluntary
inquiry, executed resource behavior and persistent inventory. Most demonstrated
effects depend on the explicitly new current-basis need projection. The unchanged
006/007 path also demonstrates one full-chain held-out condition through legitimate
redelivery and concern resolution. No retention mode wins universally; FIFO remains
the default. No Experiment011 is begun.

Full-chain classification requires matching pre-inquiry action readiness, different
immediate post-reply readiness and agreement with executed opening actions, plus
different inquiry and persistent food. Read-only diagnostics use the same local
resource observation and never influence inquiry. Hidden/late-attribution cases

differences there do not automatically qualify. This prevents overstating mediation.
It is a controlled causal diagnostic, not a proof that inquiry is the sole possible
cause under arbitrary alternative schedules.

## Held-out sensitivity, harmful choices and uncertainty

Four fixed combinations vary goals, weak/traffic/response quality, effort, traffic
count, original-source availability, meeting/delivery order, later partner and
resource amount. The minor/all-forget controls deliberately keep their stated
goal40/quality20/traffic90 overrides. Quality keeps55 against20 traffic. These are
scenario-specific isolation conditions disclosed in source, not retention inputs.
Meeting participant IDs sort canonically; reversing supplied meeting order tests
that invariance, while reversed evidence order changes receipt arrival order.
These variants reuse the same families: parameter sensitivity, not external transfer.

At goal80, weak55,48 receipts, effort0, response90 and fixed later partner2,
Quality/Salient in the quality family chooseB and transfer food while FIFO asksA
Direct and leaves. The repeated family also favors selective allocation with this
mirrored future. Salient alone transfers in the protected variant. In other
variants the same choice misses the future partner or wastes a slot. Minor FIFO
helps in two additional variants; its mirrored partner variant instead favors
selective allocation. No retention or inquiry policy changes between variants.

The unchanged-path full-chain held-out case is redelivery under that same
goal80/55/90 configuration. Retained plus newly delivered evidence legitimately
resolvesA for Salient, releasing its single slot toB. FIFO/Quality do not reach the
same closure, pursueA, and miss the fixedB resource opportunity. Pre-inquiryB
readiness matches; the selected reply creates the executed transfer difference.
Thus the old mechanism can transmit retention effects indirectly through concern
status, although it usually suppresses direct basis differences.

Hidden-root fixtures receive two Unknown reports from the same actual inverted50
acquisition plus correct40 native evidence. Local negative confidence75 produces
support−35 although the listener cannot know the shared root. Later correct90
can shift support to19 without resolving the conflict. This is mistaken retained
confidence, not a guarantee that correction erases its consequences. In the late
case, both original/relay providers disclose their actual available attribution
only after query0. Salient's retained negative groups collapse75→50, support−35→−10.
Neither the first slot nor a missed transfer is restored. FIFO receives new local
reports where older acquisitions were evicted; it does not reconstruct them.

Inverted capability responses are intentionally preserved. With weak55 and strong
negative90, retained ambiguity/partial confidence may make Salient miss the fixedA
transfer while FIFO/Quality proceed through their own subsequent local responses.
At two-unit stakes, source and partner choices can reverse which mode transfers.
There is no calibrated outcome objective that makes every transfer universally
preferable. Strong unresolved conflict and clean weakB are both legal candidates;
existing scores frequently chooseA. No alternative is scripted to be correct.

## Explanations and limits

A, interpreted as universal behavioral inertness, is falsified. B is demonstrated
by actor-local scores and different voluntary selections. C occurs in two controlled
and fifteen held-out CurrentNeed groups. D occurs in four controlled and twelve
held-out CurrentNeed groups, plus one held-out unchanged group under the conservative
criterion. E's proposed input/schedule artifacts are excluded by structural local
inputs, matched initial/delivery assertions, paired future-partner test and actual
legality/accounting. The fixed laboratory opportunities themselves remain an
external-validity limitation; this does not establish effects in naturally arising
world schedules. Hidden and post-attribution cases are not counted as strict D.

Universal selective superiority, FIFO inferiority and 'different inquiry always
changes action' are falsified. We do not claim memory alone determines behavior,
statistical independence from tokens, optimal inquiry, human-like forgetting,
consciousness or social emergence. Larger concern diversity, longer intervals and
naturally varying opportunity costs remain untested. CurrentNeed can deprioritize
a well-supported concern that soon matters; a weak minor item can do the same in a
helpful direction. Future usefulness is unknown, not a retention signal.

persistent cognitive collection is added. Projection scans at most8 concerns over

temporary copy and <=8 support triples; the existing base decision owns its prior

## Question and competing explanations

Can justified old expectations be reconsidered through legitimate local opportunities,
without an oracle, unlimited exploration or wholesale forgetting?
A: old learning/public offers suffice. B: negative learning suppresses discovery.
C: bounded reconsideration adds a distinction. D: a transient rule suffices without
extra cognition. E: hidden/future or fixture advantages explain the result, invalidating it.
Accept neither C nor broad superiority in advance.

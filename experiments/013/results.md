# Experiment 013: adaptive expectations in a changing world

**ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION.** A transient reconsideration rule can preserve learned
expectations, occasionally revisit a previously poor opportunity, discover privately
changed circumstances through a legitimate reply, and change later executed history.
It needs no additional persistent cognition beyond existing005/006/012 state. This
is narrow support for both bounded reconsideration and simpler adaptation hypotheses,
not superiority, a default change or proof that012's cells are necessary/minimal.
013 remains exploratory pending separate scientific review; foundation protection
stays001–012. No014 was started.

## Question, prior capabilities and design

Can an individual reconsider a justified expectation, discover changed circumstances
through legitimate local opportunities, and revise later behavior without privileged
information, unlimited exploration or wholesale forgetting?

006 already learns selected-response novelty and failure, and public native evidence
offers can renew value. 007 supports actual voluntary acquisition/relay and known
independence;008 gives retained Grouped assessment and revisable interpretations;
009 FIFO evicts at32;012 separately estimates retained change with context. Those
tools often suffice eventually. Old negative learning can still delay discovery or
leave unavailable improvements forever unobserved. Absence of a possible connection
is a locality limit, not a failure to magically know that the world changed.

The [specification](specification.md) preregistered A old learning suffices; B too-stable
failure expectations; C bounded reconsideration adds capability; D simpler transient
adaptation suffices; E oracle/fixture advantage invalidates the interpretation.
Time decay, a new budget/evaluation map and extra persistent clocks were rejected as
unnecessary for this question. Public-signal-only renewal already exists and is tested.

Five modes: exact Unchanged, GlobalProgress and Contextual controls; Pulse and its
simpler Ungated ablation. New modes use exactly Contextual learning/typed Experience
through its existing replaceable policy setter. They change only prospective selection:
an own006 cell with at least two attempts and expected novelty at most half its method
prior is eligible. Pulse additionally requires the concern's current own age divisible
by4; Ungated does not. Reconsideration raises benefit to at least the existing method
prior55/65 adjusted by own credibility and legitimate independence. Ordinary cost,
offers, importance, enumeration, strict ties and Pause stay unchanged. Stored estimates
are never reset; actual valid replies alone train old/new cells by their existing rules.
As in012, Unchanged also captures/updates the012 estimate without using it in selection;
it is a behavioral-use ablation, not a zero-storage control.

Age is existing005 local encounter experience, not global time, observer inquiry IDs,
a hidden source-change detector or a new persistent clock. No new ECS resource or
cognitive collection is added. The rule is pure/replaceable and installed only by013
before its first query. Default/historical modes are untouched. The half-prior threshold,
two attempts and period4 are heuristics; they were not tuned after outcomes.

## Worlds, continuity and interventions

Seven persistent agents, three concerns arising from actual refusal events1/3/5,
initial food4 each, fixed Grouped/FIFO, matched traits/profiles per seed and mode.
Core has24 meetings, longitudinal48; sensitivity extends to32/40 and56/72. Each meeting
offers one focal inquiry among competing concerns, with actual participant replies and
time costs. Resource scenes every16 meetings and at the end execute existing scarcity
policy, followed by explicit actor consumption. No resource/learning/concern reset
occurs after voluntary divergence. Consumable food is finite, and no production is invented.

13 designed families and five modes give65 core trials. Two preregistered coupled
configurations give130 sensitivity trials. Seeds0–3,42,u64 maximum give1,170 worlds,
each complete state rerun exactly. Initial generated expectation traits vary by seed
but are fixed across mode comparisons. Seeds test replay and small initial-state
variation, not independent statistical evidence.

Changes are fixed independently of treatment: provider inspection/acquisition,36
legitimate relay receipts evicting a provider acquisition, declared privacy/capability
interventions, voluntary metadata offers, and36 unrelated actor receipts causing008
forgetting. Acquisition, availability, willingness, advertised quality and actual
reply are separate. Setting a capability/profile never directly chooses a focal
question or injects its answer. The source's policy chooses observation/offer/response.

Longitudinal worlds have provider4 acquire A, provider5 acquire B, provider4 lose A
through FIFO, provider5 become unwilling and later willing, and provider6 acquire an
inverted A reading and legitimately relay it to4, who can voluntarily advertise its
new attribution. Same people/cell histories persist throughout. Already known
independence and resolved concerns can make changes irrelevant. Opportunities remain
laboratory-scheduled, not an autonomous society or naturally emergent encounters.

Pulse does not periodically correct every belief: absent sources, known independence,
low importance/high cost, competing concerns, concern closure/eviction and finite

both novelty and progress expectations. Ungated's persistent probing is a failure,
not a recommendation for unlimited exploration.

## Interpretation and handoff

Demonstrated: legitimate discovery can be accelerated without replacing stored
expectations or adding cognition; local reconsideration can alter executed persistent
history; older mechanisms often adapt; unobservable improvements remain unknowable;
mistakes/costs, actual acquisition/eviction and long-history feedback are preserved.
Narrow hypotheses C and D receive support; A also explains many later recoveries,
and B persists in unreachable or crowded-out opportunities. Oracle explanation E
is excluded at the tested boundaries, not as a proof for every possible implementation.

Mechanism-dependent: period4/half-prior/two-attempt eligibility, prior optimism,
importance/cost competition, known-independence exclusion, existing binary change
targets and old trust/memory lifetimes. Ambiguous: long-run utility, best exploration
budget, whether a different transient/episodic approach does better, isolated factor
effects and ecological prevalence. Unsupported: optimal learning, calibrated competence,
human curiosity/consciousness/general intelligence or naturally emerging civilizations.

The core positive timing depends on the fixed acquisition/resource schedule aligning
with local opportunities. The two sensitivity configurations vary multiple factors
together, not isolated period/phase or environmental volatility. Cost ticks do not
reduce the exogenous number of meetings or induce autonomous hunger/travel changes.
Continuous laboratory histories are a bridge, not evidence of natural social emergence.

The strongest unresolved question is how to reopen a plausible Evidence opportunity
without letting repeated prior-based Direct exploration crowd it out, while preserving
finite attention and the value of legitimately learned failures. No replacement formula,
default change,013 foundation contract or014 design is added here.

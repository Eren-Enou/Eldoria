# Experiment014: competing activities under scarce time

**ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION.** Existing locally retained experience can affect
activity allocation and later executed resource behavior when elapsed time removes
an alternative. Legal time constraints now change opportunities, not just audit
counters. Both beneficial and costly allocations occur. A simple priority often
matches or exceeds the local selector; neither Local nor LocalPulse is justified as
default. This experiment remains exploratory pending independent review, outside
the protected001–013 foundation. No015.

## Question, hypotheses and actual scope

Can an individual choose between information gathering and material activity when
spending time on one permanently removes the other? The hypotheses are: A, existing
cognition suffices; B, uncoordinated priorities lose opportunities; C, a local selector
changes persistent outcomes; D, simpler priorities perform comparably; E, privileged
information or fixture construction explains apparent gains. The results support narrow A–D
distinctions. Tested local boundaries exclude an oracle, but laboratory construction
limits broader interpretation and cross-activity utility is uncalibrated.

Three stationary persistent people, focal0/resource partner1/inquiry supplier2.
Two concerns originate in actual refusals by1. Reuse005 concern lifetimes,006
novelty/strategy learning,007 acquired replies,008 Grouped,009 FIFO,012 Contextual
and exact013 Pulse beneath LocalPulse. No new planning or cognitive collection.
New files implement only pure activity selection, a bounded Bevy environment

are unchanged; original151 protected artifacts remain intact.

## Exact activity/time model

At public opening, the focal individual sees its own state, own current query
projection/resource readiness and at most two current opportunity closing times.
Each window is usable once. The actor never receives the future schedule, private
provider knowledge/willingness, partner resources/traits, truth, seed, scenario
labels or observer history. Individual availability is serial: selected execution
occupies the existing shared Runtime clock, with core resource busy/legality checks.
Other people reply through existing policies; they do not independently schedule
activities in this laboratory.

Queries preserve the old voluntary target/method selection. This adapter supports
third-party acquired replies, excluding original-refuser native sensor protocols.
Direct reserves13 ticks and actually takes12/13; Evidence reserves31 and takes30/31.
One selection tick, old method10/28 and bounded response1/2 are charged. Old hunger
terms in query *score cost* are not additional elapsed ticks. Resource reserves its
public six-turn cap and actually takes1–3 in these histories. Admission requires
reservation to fit both closing time and activity horizon. Actual Runtime delta,
not reservation, is charged; no negative budget, refund or free query is introduced.

After each completion, spent/expired options disappear. Wait advances to the end
and records unused opportunities, without simulating their unchosen outcomes.
Consumption follows in three separately allocated ticks, one per participant;
total period duration is activity budget+3. It is mandatory existing explicit
consumption, not a new voluntary eating planner. Relative hunger+20 between later
periods is an explicitly recorded external intervention; there is no spontaneous
metabolism. Initial food is finite and never reset or replenished after divergence.
Setup/inspection/pressure time is recorded separately between budgeted periods.

Resource activity executes the unchanged core negotiation/interpretation/food
resolver. The isolated014 entry point excludes automatic conversation/inquiry
follow-ups and captures genuine new refusal concerns. The verifier accounts for
every post-setup resource event/query in charged spans. Budget enforcement applies
to this opt-in protocol, not arbitrary direct calls to legacy APIs.

## Negative, equal and longer-history findings

- No-information: real silence and declining estimates coexist with repeated effort;
  material cooperation can later arise without new information as hunger and the
  last-four memory window change. Do not credit all cooperation to inquiry.
- No-material: no resource window/scene or invented transfer; priorities fall back
  to inquiry or Wait. Resource priority cannot create a partner opportunity.
- Improvement: the supplier's midway acquisition is real and initially private;
  core queries may discover it, but low-budget/quality configuration records zero
  query changes despite the new knowledge. Pulse does not guarantee discovery.
- Willingness/mirrored: old cooperation can turn into refusal; source knowledge
  may be retained but withheld. Equal current inputs preserve the first choice.
  Silence never diagnoses why a source failed to answer.
- Tight/reservation: query13 leaves too little for uniform resource reserve6, even
  though a matched broad-budget resource interaction actually takes2. Conservative
  reservation protects legality but can reject feasible fast outcomes. The six-turn
  bound is not a measured six-turn duration or an optimal/tight protocol bound.
- Loose: more time permits both activities when their closing windows also allow
  it; it does not rescue a resource deadline already passed or manufacture information.
- Longitudinal24–40 periods: same agents, stock, trust, concerns and learning persist.
  Real provider and actor FIFO pressure and later focal inspection/relay are recorded.
  The latter is external acquired knowledge, not a budgeted-inquiry discovery.
  Core Local/ResourceFirst execute3 transfers, InquiryFirst/NoDeadline2; all finish
  with zero food and hunger100. Finite stock exhaustion is not sustainable survival.

Assessment eviction does not erase every belief, provenance, concern, estimate or
relationship consequence. Preserve separate lifetimes; no observer recovery occurs.
Original observations remain immutable even after provider eviction.

## Complexity, autonomy and unsupported conclusions

New environment state has one bounded current clock/window record, no per-agent
planning history. Existing cognitive caps remain. Serialized012 learning maps span
2–455 bytes across primary840 rows; complete trials116585–2721291 bytes. Observer
activity checkpoints and old getters copy growing history; serialized bytes are
not resident memory. No general infrastructure optimization was performed.

Five repeats of20000 identical-input calls give median activity-selector times
approximately761/768/768/773/764ns (ResourceFirst/InquiryFirst/Local/NoDeadline/
LocalPulse). Local projection median2375ns exceeds selection here; it includes
bounded own-state copying and old scoring. These exclude resolution and updates.
Whole-suite timing includes construction/causal validation across diverging histories,
not isolated per-policy overhead; history getters/copies are visible possible costs,
not a profiled attribution. Existing throughput is checked separately.

Demonstrated: real elapsed time removes opportunities; current local cognition and
availability affect selection; legitimate replies can alter executed cooperation;
costly/misleading/no-information choices persist; clocks and food balance reproduce.
Mechanism-dependent: priority weights25/hunger/2, conservative caps, one-use windows,
third-party protocol, old memory/learning/tie rules and deadline scaffolds.
Ambiguous: net value of the selector, tighter safe bounds, transfer welfare, all-pair
mediation, endogenous world prevalence and isolated cost/schedule effects.
Unsupported: optimal survival/economic behavior, psychological realism, general
intelligence, autonomous society, natural institutions or sustainable ecology.

Environmental autonomy advances only at the allocation boundary: agents choose
activities and responses within externally published opportunities whose time now
matters. Meetings/windows, hunger interventions and consumption phases remain
laboratory supplied; there is no movement, production or self-generated encounter
system. The strongest open question is whether a minimal locally justified activity
rule still helps when safe costs and opportunity generation are less laboratory
aligned, without letting nested inquiry choose an unaffordable method or adding a
general planner. No default adoption or014 protection is justified by this report.

# Experiment021 results — Local Environmental Knowledge

**ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION**, as a bounded research result; **unprotected and
unadopted**. The protected foundation remains001–020. This is an executed214-world
study, not evidence of active exploration, general environmental understanding or
a continuously functioning community. No022 was begun.

## Question and fixed treatment

Can legitimately acquired, bounded and potentially outdated environmental
information change voluntary activity and subsequent physical/social history when
other actors change the environment without updating one's knowledge?

The [specification](specification.md), [design gate](design-gate.md) and214-row
[matrix](registered-matrix.json) were published in `ee1eb6bd15c2573b39ab3e3ae9109765efc9cdca`
before implementation/outcomes. No post-outcome policy, capacity, weight, cadence,
lifetime, trait, opportunity or physical-rule tuning occurred. A source hash before
the matrix run and a separately documented whitespace-only rustfmt change preserve
that distinction. Independent-reader/presentation corrections did not change worlds.

One owned slot stores a dated local observation and the owner's subsequent take
count. Quantity is a last-seen upper bound, not magically current stock. Rival
grants never update it. Own success updates only a still-available record and never
refreshes its date. Expired quantity is not recovered from the live attempt or
observer archive; physical food can survive its loss. A failed own claim supplies
a new empty experience. Sensor redelivery is a new owner-local receipt.

CurrentOnly, LastSeen and Expire12 share fixed passive glance opportunities and
identical initial physical/cognitive state. FreshReference senses at every free
choice and Unobserved disables the channel: these are frequency/channel controls,
not isolated memory contrasts. Chosen work can cause a later fixed glimpse to be
missed. The policy never receives future glance slots, stock without a receipt,
hidden rivals' possessions, future relevance or observer records.

## Observation versus interpretation

Demonstrated: owned dated knowledge changes eligibility and actual execution;
stale positives can waste the full duration; material/social consequences can
persist across windows; endpoint material reconvergence does not erase history;
forgetting does not imply erasing separately established food/trust/consequences.

Mechanism-dependent: treating a positive last-seen upper bound as sufficient to try,
Need/Acquire's inherited scores and ties, passive absolute glance slots, one-slot
overwrite and age12 expiry. The selector does not explicitly reason about staleness
risk, unknown sources or the expected value of refreshing information.

Ambiguous: how much of the particular ongoing advantage comes from the invitation
heuristic's later social dynamics versus acquisition timing. The first divergence
is isolated, but all later mediators are allowed to interact. A lower hunger area
is one model statistic, not a universal good. Cost-free continuous sensing is an
unfair basis for claiming memory efficiency; sensing cost was not modeled.

Unsupported: improved survival, active curiosity, exploration, calibrated beliefs,
general planning, psychological realism, an emergent community, natural social
environments, renewed supply, spatial knowledge, multiple sources or Eldoria canon.
Stale-negative-to-positive correction is impossible with monotonic stock/fixed
access here; overwrite/new receipt is tested, but reopening a depleted source is not.

## Complexity, status and next question

One cognitive slot per actor is bounded; receipt counters contain no event content.
Memory audit records and old physical/cognitive histories grow and are observer-only.
The new adapter duplicates a private frozen lifecycle instead of refactoring020;
this is a maintenance cost, not a new general scheduler. Quantitative wall-clock,
serialization/compression and throughput results are in [verification](verification.md).
No general optimization was performed.

The result justifies keeping this isolated mechanism as research evidence. It does
not justify global adoption or protecting021 within this program. A separate review
should challenge the passive sensing/scheduling confound and compare the benefit
with a simpler current sensor before adoption. The strongest next question is
whether information continuity still has useful consequences when observing is a
voluntary, costly alternative to work or contact. That question requires its own
prospective gate; this program implements no022.

## Qualifications that do not become demonstrated findings

Passive observation at externally declared slots is an experimental channel,not
general attention,vision or costly exploration. No opportunity schedule is exposed
to the selector;post-choice busy states can legitimately miss later fixed slots.
CurrentOnly is a serious non-memory alternative. FreshReference supplies more sensing
and cannot establish an isolated retention benefit. No renewed/newly accessible
negative-source correction can occur under monotonic finite stock;do not fake it.

The old atomic-query exclusivity remains. General concurrency and the original
unexercised provisional-query blocking branch require actual witnesses before claims.
Duplicated private lifecycle code is an architectural cost,not an invitation to
rewrite protected helpers. Three stationary actors/one cache do not demonstrate a
community. If implementation cannot meet conservation,time,locality or no-recovery
without changing frozen semantics,stop and report before forcing the experiment.

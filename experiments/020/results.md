# Experiment020: Finite Environmental Resource Acquisition

Scientific decision: **ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION**. All required checks passed;see
[verification](verification.md) and the [completion audit](delivery-audit.md). This is an experimental result,
not an independent scientific review, default adoption or extension of001–019.
The preregistered [specification](specification.md) and [design gate](design-gate.md)
were committed/pushed as `6c75e790456ef5d1559a114f9bce2ba6a87c1ded` before treatment code.
The preceding viewer milestone was separately pushed as `831d8fc`.

The tested question is whether a finite, locally observed environmental food source
can change independently selected activity, competition and subsequent individual
and interpersonal histories beyond redistribution/consumption of initially owned
food. The answer is yes in these controlled fixtures. Acquisition changes who owns
and consumes food, can support an actual voluntary transfer, and can also prevent
help for a losing claimant. It does not establish a generally better policy,
sustainable survival, naturally occurring cooperation or welfare superiority.

## Physical mechanism and local boundary

One stationary co-present CacheId0 has finite initial stock, no replenishment,
production, decay or travel. A legal attempt costs four global ticks in the primary
configuration; two/eight are held-out sensitivities. All free actors choose before
any new claim reserves its actor. No unit is reserved. Completion rechecks current
stock; checked arithmetic debits one cache unit and credits one owned unit, or
changes neither balance. Simultaneous completions use the registered ascending,
descending or rotating proposer rank and recorded submission wave. Arbitration
does not see hunger, welfare, future usefulness or inquiry content.

Every captured state and independent operation reconstruction checks:

`initial owned food + initial cache = current owned food + current cache + meals`.

An existing meal consumes one owned unit and reduces hunger by40, bounded below at0.
The unchanged018 clock adds five hunger per four elapsed ticks, bounded above at100.
Resource action, due grant and already selected other actor's meal occur in that
order within a shared tick. Concurrent work never adds a second global tick.
Claimants cannot choose another activity, eat, respond to invitations or enter a
resource scene until completion. Failed claimants pay the entire duration.

The pure selector receives the actor's existing bounded/local encounter input,
current public cache observation, duration and a public atomic-inquiry availability
flag. It receives no scenario label, seed, future stock, future partner, actual
response, objective event archive, hidden provenance or partner-private state.
The new cache observation is ephemeral; acquisition adds no cognitive learning,
cache memory or changed retention. All old cognitive caps and lifetimes remain.
Own inventory legitimately changes after a successful grant.

Inquiry remains the existing prospective Contextual mechanism with Grouped
assessment and unchanged retention. Atomic inquiry cannot leap over a live claim's
deadline: pending claims mask only Inquiry contact candidates; a newly admitted
claim can block a provisional same-cohort query with an explicit reason. No fallback
or learning is invented. All actual queries occur without pending claims. None of
the registered worlds exercised the provisional-query blocking branch; ordinary
query execution and changed query allocation were exercised. This declared
serialization boundary limits concurrency claims.

## Policies and matched controls

The old Baseline activity ranking is unchanged. Need appends eligible acquisition
with score equal to current own hunger; strict first maximum preserves old ties.
Acquire prioritizes an eligible acquisition; otherwise it uses the same old ranking.
Acquisition requires own food0, hunger>0, current visible positive accessible stock,
own availability and enough remaining window for the declared duration.

Five worlds per primary family distinguish supply placement from access and choice:

| Mode | Initial supply placement | Cache access / acquisition policy |
|---|---|---|
| Owned | Cache units distributed cyclically into initial inventories | Empty cache; Ignored |
| Closed | Same initial owned food and finite cache as other cache worlds | Closed; Ignored |
| Ignored | Same physical placement | Open; keeps the old activity selection |
| Need | Same physical placement | Open; current hunger score |
| Acquire | Same physical placement | Open; acquisition priority |

Total potential food is identical within every matched group. Closed/Ignored/Need/
Acquire also match initial owned food, hunger, profiles, private state, historical
cognition and external windows. Owned changes placement and immediate availability;
it is not a pure access or acquisition-cost ablation. Post-choice divergence is
carried forward without resets. No weights, profiles, thresholds or durations were
retuned after outcomes. Extreme generosity/caution are declared initial fixture
conditions, not post-acquisition interventions or commanded actions; they limit
generality of the sharing diagnostic.

One metadata qualification:Owned retains the family's nominal cache capacity while
starting with stock0 after placing those units in inventories. The specification's
phrase “capacity equal to initial stock” does not literally describe Owned's
post-placement frame;it does describe the four cache-bearing controls. Capacity
does not enter selection or create/refill food. The recorded capacity and original
specification remain unchanged;this does not supply an additional material resource.

## Strongest positive chain: sharing

Primary record33,Need:at tick0 actors0 and1 independently select acquisition of the
same single unit; actor2 waits. Both finish at4. Ascending rank grants actor0 and
actor1's stale positive observation legitimately ends in Depleted. The loser is not
silently awarded food or refunded time. At4 both actors select resource contact.

Existing resource policy makes actor0 Offer at7,11 and15. The earlier offers are
refused; these inconvenient negotiations remain. Actor1 finally Accepts at16,
event5/scene2, transferring the only unit from0 to1. Actor1 consumes it at17,
`periods[0].steps[13].activity.consumption[0]`, hunger100→60. Its changed hunger
persists into subsequent states. No donor, recipient or Accept action was scripted.
The initial sole cache unit and zero owned inventory identify its material origin
without inventing fungible-unit identities.

Closed record31 has no food transfer or meal. Need changes actor1's hunger-area
from14200 to13520; actor0's11680 and actor2's10200 remain unchanged. The result
demonstrates a real social consequence, not that sharing was optimal:descending
ordering grants the hungry actor1 directly, producing no transfer and lower actor1
area13040. An Offer/Accept count is not a welfare measure.

## Strongest negative chain: lost_contact

Primary Closed record26,descending order:actor1's voluntary invitation to0 executes,
Offer event0 at3 and Accept event1 at4 transfer the initially owned unit. Actor0
later eats. Need/Acquire records28/29 instead have actors0 and2 choose the cache at0.
Both are unavailable at the response opportunity; actor1's preserved invitation
to0 is Unavailable. Actor1 eats its own unit at3. At4 descending rank grants actor2
and actor0 loses after paying all four ticks. The missed donor encounter cannot be
restored by a later correction or a guessed fallback.

Closed meals by actor are[1,0,0];acquisition meals are[0,1,1]. Actor0 area worsens
13600→14200, while actor1 and2 improve11680→10260 and14200→13040. This is a
distributional loss for0 with gains elsewhere; it does not support a global ranking.
The lost material opportunity and failed effort are actual history, even though
all three actors eventually reach hunger100 after depletion.
Occupancy blocks the invitation before the outcome is known. Depleted then leaves
the claimant without replacement food;future failure knowledge did not cause the
earlier decision.

At duration2 the first response is still absent while claimants are busy; completion
at arbitration cannot invent earlier consent. Later legitimate contact does recover
a transfer, with actor0 area13560. At duration8 the missed help persists and two
failures occur. Duration changes scheduling, not merely a scalar utility penalty.

## Unequal access, missed opportunities and inquiry

The registered stranded hypothesis was only partly realized. Under open Ignored,
two cache units remain unused despite need. Need eventually collects both, but the
previous social dyad leaves actor1 free to acquire/consume twice:meals[0,2,0],area
[57600,55320,57600]. Acquire starts all three immediately,granting0 and1:meals[1,1,0],
area[56920,57000,57600]. Actor0 improves and actor1 worsens. Neither policy leaves
cache units unused in this primary family; do not call its Need outcome permanent
open-cache stranding. In last_window/duration8,positive stock is physically present
but no attempt fits the four-tick windows; the unit remains stranded by admission
geometry. These differ from Ignored's deliberate legacy-policy control.

In contact_bias,Need initially prefers social contact despite eligible acquisition.
The unoccupied actor2 obtains both units;Acquire instead gives0 and1 a unit each.
This is an explicit local choice/ordering effect,not an undisclosed allocation.
The private_helpful/private_cautious opening observation and decision for actor0
are identical despite different hidden recipient traits. Later cooperation differs
only after actual encounters.

Information primary Closed makes one query and nine transferred units;Need/Acquire
make two queries and five transferred units,with12 versus10 meals. A response alone
does not establish useful learning:the reader records actual retained-assessment
outcomes separately in each inquiry detail. Changed hunger,availability,wave/concern
age and history can mediate changed inquiry;020 does not isolate a novel cognitive
valuation mechanism. All query decisions are reconstructed from retained local
evidence and learned cells. Acquisition never reads truth to choose a question.

Across all22 queries,11 acquire a receipt and change retained support/status;11 do
neither. [Inquiry observations](inquiry-observations.json) retain every case. All
executed methods are Direct. Evidence's inherited33-tick contact reservation cannot
fit these24- or4-tick windows,so this is not an unconstrained method comparison or
evidence that a new valuation policy prefers cheap Direct. This admission limitation
is common to all modes and was not changed to manufacture acquisition effects.

## Longitudinal depletion and comparison with019

Ongoing Owned is an exact019 Baseline control:the targeted test matches every
recorded activity,need record and complete final snapshot. Its owned food exhausts
at258 and65 later completed zero-transfer scenes reproduce the motivating observation.

| Primary ongoing world | Meals by actor | Transfers | Cache depleted | Owned food permanently exhausted | Later completed zero-transfer scenes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Owned | [7,17,12] | 43 | Empty initially | 258 | 65 |
| Closed / Ignored | [4,12,8] | 26 | Never;12 inaccessible/ignored | 165 | 85 |
| Need / Acquire | [8,16,12] | 38 | 84 | 213 | 74 |

Owned and acquisition worlds consume the same36 potential units with different
distribution,timing and encounters. Need has lower area for0/2 but higher for1 than
Owned;available access is not uniformly preferable to initial ownership. All finish
with no owned food and hunger100. The finite cache changes trajectories and pushes
exhaustion later than Closed; it does not end repetitive empty negotiations.
The unchanged resource-contact formula is at least`H/2−2`,because its underlying
legal actions include Leave with score0. Thus a legal contact still scores at least48
at hunger100 even with no owned food and unfavorable history. This source-level
property helps explain continued contact after depletion;020 did not ablate it or
establish that removing it would improve behavior. Observer knowledge that everyone
has no food must not be substituted for an actor's legitimate uncertain experience.

Held-out ordering redistributes gains. Need ongoing descending meals differ from
ascending and transfers rise38→47;rotating gives34. Duration8 Need and Acquire
diverge despite identical four-tick primary ongoing histories. Duration2 ongoing
also has worse hunger-area than four ticks for every actor in these inputs; faster
acquisition is not monotonically better because it changes scheduling/meal timing.
All variations use frozen policies,not fitted thresholds or independent statistics.

## Limits,verification and further work

| Registered hypothesis | Finding within these fixtures |
|---|---|
| H1 replaces an activity | Demonstrated:12 primary Closed/Need contrasts |
| H2 changes later consumption | Demonstrated:executed meals and distribution |
| H3 changes sharing/negotiation | Demonstrated:sharing record33 and missed help record29 |
| H4 denies a valid claim | Demonstrated:67 stale depleted failures in the full matrix |
| H5 failure costs opportunities | Demonstrated:lost_contact,with duration sensitivity |
| H6 delays but cannot sustain consumption | Demonstrated ongoing delay versus Closed;finite available food still exhausts;not a theorem that every policy must empty every cache |
| H7 repeated exhausted-source pursuit | Not testable here:current visible zero is ineligible by construction |
| H8 harm/missed help/stranding | Individual harm and missed help demonstrated;open Need stranding primary hypothesis not realized;Ignored and unaffordable windows leave stock |
| H9 score/admission/order dominate | Sensitivity demonstrated;general dominance or a variance attribution is not established |
| H10 effects are inert | Rejected as a universal account of these fixtures;two full primary null families remain |

The evidence also rejects uniform individual improvement and monotonic improvement
from shorter acquisition duration in this matrix. It does not falsify untested
learning policies,alternative environments or long-term behavior outside this horizon.

Demonstrated:finite grants and costly failures under one clock;local independent
choice;deterministic contention;actual transfer and later meal;missed help;unequal
allocation;bounded old cognition;longitudinal reconvergence after finite depletion.

Mechanism-dependent:Need's hunger score,Acquire's priority,food0 eligibility,current
visible stock,three actors and one cache,handshake timing,strict ties,initial profile
extremes and fixed windows. Known-empty acquisition is structurally ineligible,not
learned avoidance. No new learning about failed acquisition was tested. No open Need
world with an affordable eventual attempt left food permanently unused in the
registered stranded primary case. Atomic-query overlap prevention is architectural,
with no blocked provisional query in this matrix.

Ambiguous:how much of each later cognitive/relationship change is mediated by phase,
concern age,missed encounters or different ownership. Owned controls placement and
immediate supply;they do not isolate only time cost. These hand-constructed diagnostic
worlds cannot estimate prevalence in a naturally evolving population.

Unsupported:sustainable ecology,optimal foraging,a general planner,human motivation,
broad emergence,universal welfare improvement,truth-aware selection or a justified
default policy. No travel,factions,institutions,economy,newlanguage or021 was added.

The strongest next question is how a bounded individual should value the opportunity
cost of acquisition when current food/hunger do not reveal a donor's willingness or
future availability,and how retained experience should affect continued pursuit of
unproductive contact. This experiment exposes that limitation;it does not supply or
adopt a replacement formula. Independent scientific review and any later foundation
protection remain separate tasks.

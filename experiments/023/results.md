## Scientific classification and limits

Demonstrated: persistent four-person execution, strict report crossings, local
selection/consent, one clock, accounting, causal differences, mistakes and nulls.
Mechanism-dependent: contact/consumption ranking, period-specific gating, Contextual
inquiry preference, conservative exclusive query admission and current observation
phase. Ambiguous: comprehensive personal/social benefit, effects outside the small
fixed matrix, and how much terminal need difference would persist under continued
post-stop choice. Unsupported: human realism/consciousness, autonomous society,
natural group formation, larger populations, optimal policy and sustainable resources.

H0 is false as a universal description of these fixtures but preserved in one null.
H1 holds. H2holds only as added/earlier opportunities and mixed individual gains,
not a global benefit. H3/H4costs and extra unproductive contact occur. H5temporary
reconvergence occurs. H6scarcity persists, with unused held food adding a valuation
problem. H7contact ranking/exclusive inquiry remain strong constraints. H8cannot
explain the result as *no important behavioral effects*, though engineering/audit
cost remains real. No policy or physical law was tuned to obtain these findings.

The strongest open question is how bounded local action values should account for
ongoing invitations, meals and material work when the experiment no longer forces
quiet feeding intervals. More supply alone cannot answer it. Separate independent
023review is the next gate; material preparation remains a later authorized study.

# Experiment027 — score-only null result

Scientific interpretation: **NO EXECUTED BEHAVIORAL EFFECT under the registered
learner and finite matrix.** Personal experience changed retained expectations
and eligible preparation scores, but changed no selected activity or executed
consequence. No adoption or001–027 protection follows. This is not evidence that
experience can never affect behavior.

## Where the chain stops

Across all50 Experience worlds:129 delivered results (83 Taken,46 Insufficient),
109 updates change the retained-frequency expectation. There are133 eligible
Prepare sites;58 have retained feedback and receive+10. At56, Prepare already
wins. At the other two, source21 actor0 at25/27, Prepare4→14 still loses Invite43.
There is no changed tie or integer-arithmetic defect explaining the null.

Actual memory pressure occurred:seven insertion-FIFO evictions,114 expirations
before terminal export and eight at terminal. Every cell is eventually gone;
129−7−114−8=0. At64 decision sites the actor retains outcome feedback but lacks
raw-source knowledge; Prepare remains ineligible. Observer records do not restore it.

No eligible retained-feedback site contains failure. Failure receipt, failed work
and retained negative/mixed expectation exist, but their influence on subsequent
eligible work is untested. Failure sensing legitimately updates stock knowledge
at the depleted finite source. This coverage limit was published before treatment.

## Supported and unsupported conclusions

Demonstrated: legitimate outcome delivery, bounded personal retention, fallible
frequency updates, changed local valuation, real expiry/eviction, no stock recovery,
and an exact selected/executed null across the entire registered comparison.

Mechanism-dependent: cap2/24ticks, neutral50 and±10 are uncalibrated heuristics.
The current score margins and post-failure eligibility suppress behavioral effects.
The experiment does not separate all possible learners from all possible worlds.

Missing coverage: failure-based caution at later eligible work, personal-use
feedback, beneficial/harmful learning-induced choices, material reconvergence after
learned divergence. Food is fungible; no preparation-to-meal or recipient-use
identity is invented. Higher production or transfers would not establish welfare.

Unsupported: optimality, general utility understanding, psychological realism,
general intelligence, sustainable production or broadly emergent social benefit.
The registered rule is unchanged; no thresholds were retuned after seeing nulls.

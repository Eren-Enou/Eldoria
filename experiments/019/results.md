# Experiment 019: Robust Activity Priorities and Opportunity Timing

**ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION as research. No tested policy is justified as a global
default.** Small score perturbations usually preserve terminal material state, but
occasionally change real contacts, information and persistent consequences. Public
windows and idle observation cadence also change outcomes substantially in their
tested families. The new Deadline rule makes a genuine distinction from EatFirst,
without demonstrating a general advantage. Some apparent pressure-time gains arise
from delayed eating after an unproductive encounter, rather than better cooperation.

An independent scientific review remains separate future work.019 is not added to
foundation protection. No020 is begun. All valid negative and ambiguous outcomes remain.

under window6 cannot legally offer resource contact because its public bound is8.

## Scientific classification and remaining question

Demonstrated:some score and priority differences propagate into actual encounters,
information,resource behavior and persistent states;many perturbations are inert at
terminal endpoints. Timing can independently alter opportunities and meal efficiency.
Conservative admission can exclude an interaction that actually fits. Negative and
reconvergent effects are real findings.

Mechanism-dependent:top-level10% integer scaling,old contact/query collapse,strict
ties,EatFirst priority,last-safe-slot rule,public upper bounds,synchronized pulse/meal
geometry,cohort aging,seeded expectations,Continue0 and finite stocks. Ambiguous:
relative causal contribution to late area,optimal decision cadence,grounded contact
value,which seed traits explain a reversal,and ecological frequency. Unsupported:
expected-utility optimality,rational welfare maximization,realistic psychology,fairness,
sustainability,human-like intelligence or general social emergence.

The strongest remaining question is whether effects survive externally grounded
opportunity windows and independently justified food/contact valuations. The current
data do not justify answering it by retuning this completed experiment. A separate
independent019 review should precede adoption/protection or another behavioral study.

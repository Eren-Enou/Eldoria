# Experiment 007 hypothesis and tradeoffs

Written before behavior changes, 2026-10-07. Preserve all earlier defaults,
schemas and evidence. Opt in after Inquiry; use a separate ECS resource and
replaceable pure policies, with stable domain IDs and integer scores.

Hypothesis: distinct locally known observation tokens permit corroboration;
known common tokens suppress redundant relay value. Unknown attribution remains
a speaker-based assumption and can cause overconfidence. Later voluntary
attribution replaces that assumption, reducing confidence through the existing
belief/memory/ordered trust revision machinery.

Mechanism: a recorded, voluntary instrumented inspection of a refuser's historical
scarcity record gives each selected inspector one immutable fallible observation.
The refuser must voluntarily open that narrow channel. This is retrospective
inspection, not magically witnessing a past event. Inspectors choose before the
resolver samples. Structured voluntary exchanges copy only a provider's bounded
acquisition; objective audit links the real root and parent receipt separately.
Attribution availability is an explicit channel intervention. Two intermediate
relays suffice; no autonomous propagation. Local acquisitions/received evidence
are bounded at 32; evictions are FIFO and can erase lineage knowledge.

Confidence is a bounded heuristic: group locally believed lineages, take strongest
positive/negative quality per group, combine independent groups by residual
support (q plus remaining uncertainty times q/100), then subtract opposing
support. Thus independence is not truth. Speaker identity and lineage are distinct.
Credibility weights transmitted quality, never supplies independence. Repeated
known evidence has zero realized value; independent corroboration adds value.

candidate enumeration. Apply a local expected-independence factor before selecting
one inquiry; learn from its actual voluntary response. No private provenance,
sensor outcome, seed or channel in policy inputs. Keep old paths untouched when
the resource is absent. Extend the receipt resolver only with an explicit private
support override and actual communicator field so old serialization stays exact.

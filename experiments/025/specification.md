## Question and alternatives

Will four persistent individuals voluntarily spend real time on a locally known
but uncertain finite material-preparation opportunity, and what physical,
informational, interpersonal and later behavioral consequences follow?

H0: eligible work is ignored under existing contact values. H1: legitimate local
knowledge permits voluntary work. H2: stale knowledge produces costly unsuccessful
competition. H3: successful work yields usable food without later use. H4: later
independently selected meals or consensual transfers propagate consequences.
H5: physical access changes behavior but worsens some hunger/social outcomes.
H6: uncertainty, effort, one-slot overwrite, timing or inquiry exclusion suppress
usefulness. H7: physical food trajectories reconverge while history differs. H8:
finite material exhausts; repeated opportunities need not establish continuing
benefit. Negative and missing witnesses are valid results; no success quota.

## Physical ontology and exact law

One stationary RawSourceId0 is a distinct tagged identity from ReadyCacheId0. It
physically exists initially in every matched world, even with closed access.
Each raw/food/waste/consumed unit has the same conserved accounting mass.
Raw already contains an edible fraction: **2raw→1edible+1inert waste** separates it;
it does not create matter, energy or new nutrients. Waste remains in the ledger.
No tools, plants, farms, property law, resource renewal or raw trading are modeled.

At every primitive operation, including mid-process:

`M_initial = raw_source + sum(raw_escrow) + ready_cache + sum(owned_food) + waste + consumed`

All additions/subtractions and deadline arithmetic are checked; no negative/wrapped
account, discarded remainder or output overfill. Validated starting stocks bound
all possible future ownership. Existing meals debit one food and credit one consumed
unit, reducing modeled hunger by40 with floor zero. Hunger+5/4ticks, cap100 is an
inherited abstraction, not a caloric/health/death model. No indefinite survival claim.

## One voluntary commitment, two actual phases

Prepare competes with Wait, Consume, Invite and Acquire. Inquiry remains within
the old invitation path. At admission require actor availability, legitimate public
access, candidate eligibility, duration fit and food output room; actual raw stock
must not gate policy or reveal future success. Reserve the actor for the full sum
of positive extraction and preparation durations. At most one active batch/actor;
all commitments share the old authoritative busy/availability law.

At extraction deadline revalidate SourceId0 and actual stock. If stock>=2, atomically
debit2source and credit2owned escrow. Otherwise move no material and record failure.
Each attempt remains occupied until its original full completion deadline, even
when extraction fails. No refunded time or reassignment to a different activity.

At completion success debits2actual escrow, credits1owned food and1waste, records
the outcome and releases exactly once. Failure credits neither. No food can be
eaten/transferred before completion. No automatic meal, gift, negotiation, trust
change or next preparation follows. Later actions remain independently selected.

At nominal stop during extraction, all raw remains at source; after extraction,
owned escrow remains raw. Snapshots preserve it, not premature output. Admitted
work completes through the charged drain. No cancellation, interruption, spoilage
or arbitrary cleanup law is added. An insufficient odd remainder remains raw.

projects the current tag; RawSource projects None. A disjoint backing representation,
if needed for old-source reuse, must assert combined capacity<=1 and export one

## Exact local candidate heuristic and policy isolation

Primary valuation is fixed **Baseline**, not selected after seeing preparation.
MealRelief stays available under its exact original meaning for historical parity;
there is no secondary policy factorial or pooled preparation/policy effect here.

Preserve all four old final scores and masks. Append Prepare **after Acquire** so
ties cannot displace any old equal-score candidate. Prepare eligibility requires
own food0, hunger>0, own current RawSource record with date<=now, remembered
stock−own removals>=2, remembered public access enabled, own public availability,
and checked now+known total duration<=public end. Feature-off disables the candidate.
Ineligible score−1. Eligible score is exactly:

`Prepare = own_hunger − (known_extraction_ticks + known_preparation_ticks)`

This deliberately simple heuristic retains the old Need scale for acquiring one
food unit, with a one-point-per-occupied-tick penalty. Yield is fixed one, no
quality/probability/long-run multipliers. The effort penalty is a prospective
heuristic convention, not calibrated utility or optimal duration normalization.
It may undervalue work, be crowded by contact, or pursue stale stock. Do not tune
it or add a survival override after results. Integer arithmetic and first strict
maximum over Wait,Consume,Invite,Acquire,Prepare are exact.

Implement a pure replaceable function over owned food/hunger, owned tagged record,
public now/end/availability and the four unchanged local candidate terms. No hidden
stock, rival inventory/escrow/intent, future hunger, partners, responses, success,
scenario label, experiment access label or observer outcome enters selection.
The legitimately received public access flag is part of the affordance; external
configuration is not passed as a shortcut around that observation.

A live outgoing invitation masks duplicate Invite under old law; it does not
reserve the proposer for the future accepted encounter. Preserve that distinction.
An available actor may select Prepare during a pending protocol, just as old
ordinary consumption can occur then. Later admission must recheck availability
and may fail. Do not add a new promise-honoring rule or reserve a future outcome.

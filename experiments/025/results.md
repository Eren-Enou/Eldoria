# Experiment025 — voluntary finite material preparation

## Finding: narrow positive result, with material qualifications

Four bounded individuals can voluntarily turn observed finite raw material into
food through real, exclusive, two-phase work; subsequently chosen meals and social
resource exchanges can use that output. Preparation changes executed trajectories
in21of24 access comparisons. It also causes unsuccessful attempts, opportunity
costs and a worse hunger trajectory for one individual in a matched comparison.
This is finite preparation under controlled opportunities, not renewable supply,
sustainable survival, a general planner or an emergent productive community.

All50 registered worlds now execute under the separately frozen **observer2**
validation successor. The original failed attempt remains preserved. No policy,
physical law, retention rule, capacity, scenario or registered parameter was
retuned after outcomes. [Verification](verification.md) explains both observer
corrections and the read-only evidence gate. No adoption or025foundation extension.

## Design and causal contrast

[Registration](specification.md) and the24-question [gate](design-gate.md) were
published atb2487dc before implementation. The fixed matrix contains24access-only
pairs and2reporting placebos:16controlled pairs and8held-out parameter variations.
Every primary arm uses the unchanged Baseline activity valuation. Initial raw
material, other resources, actors, observations and external schedules match;
accessibility is the intervention. Worlds may diverge after voluntary choices.

The appended local Prepare score is own hunger minus known total work duration,
eligible only with no owned food, a retained accessible raw observation and enough
remembered material/time. Old action ties win. The actor does not see actual rival
debits or future usefulness. A single environmental slot holds raw or ready-cache
knowledge. Capacity, old inquiry heuristics, consent and food accounting stay fixed.

The physical law is2raw→1food+1inert waste. At actual extraction, stock is checked
and debited into owned escrow; conversion happens only after further effort.
Failures pay the full registered interval. Common units satisfy
`raw + escrow + ready cache + owned food + waste + consumed = initial mass`.
This is bookkeeping in symbolic material units, not an energy or nutrition model.

## Sharing can be real without being universally beneficial

Record17(actor2, commitment12) starts from its legitimate tick6 raw receipt. At10,
Prepare19 beats Invite10. It extracts at12, completes at16 and owns1new food unit.
At16, Invite120 beats the eligible Consume30. Existing voluntary resource policy
then offers food; actor3 chooses Accept35 over Refuse−34. Actual event13 at20
transfers the unit: balances[1,2]→[0,3]. The donor's prepared unit is isolated to
this debit; the recipient's later meal cannot be assigned to that specific unit
because it already held2food. Actor3 eats at22, hunger15→0.

This pair has the actor2 hunger-area cost+2040 above. It demonstrates a costly
access-enabled trajectory with real sharing, not that this transfer alone explains
the entire area difference or that the alternative was globally optimal. A proposal
blocked while preparation occupied the actor is also recorded, but a blocked
proposal is not proof of a useful or mutually accepted counterfactual interaction.

## Non-use, competing work, inquiry and persistence

Five completed outputs remain unused through the evaluation end, but none has a
later genuine ordinary meal option. These are late/drain outputs, **not evidence
of voluntary persistent neglect**. In8worlds an eligible Prepare candidate loses
to another activity. Record17's Invite-over-Consume is a genuine later choice;
it must not be confused with having no later opportunity.

Executed inquiries occur in records19,20and21. Their existing source/method logic
and local learning are reconstructed; no new inquiry planner was introduced.
Atomic inquiry does not advance across active preparation deadlines. The registered
drain settles commitments, and reporting checkpoints can cut across actual work.

Enabled siblings5/7(order variation) reconverge in full material at7, while trust
and hunger-area histories differ. Siblings1/25(effort variation) reach the same
terminal material, but longer work adds240hunger-area units to each actor. Thus
material reconvergence does not erase executed social or hunger history.

## Negative results and fixture limitations

Sibling5/9(the rotating final-batch variation) is inert in the realized schedule.
The Absolute-phase case also starts at0, so its phase difference is inert. These
are not robustness demonstrations. Seed repetitions are deterministic sensitivity
cases, not independent statistical samples or external transfer evidence.

RawOnly visibility deliberately omits ready-cache sightings in both primary arms.
The access-closed comparison is therefore not old-world feature-off parity. Source
visits are fixed controlled observations, not autonomous travel. Importance, source
knowledge and food use are not learned optimal values. Stronger work costs and
social opportunities are only the registered alternatives, not an exhaustive search.

No case establishes unused food despite an eventual voluntary meal opportunity.
No new test was invented after seeing outcomes to manufacture that missing witness.
Current output is finite, no renewable productive economy is modeled, and retained
source information remains a fallible local upper bound.

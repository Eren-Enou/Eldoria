# Experiment017: Endogenous Opportunities and Changing Social Availability

**ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATION as a research result.** Actual independently chosen
resource commitments change current public availability. A free person's purposeful
consumption during those commitments can change later invitations, executed transfers,
relationships and persistent material state. Commitments alone produce identical
completed worlds to serial execution in this three-person platform. The added material
effect is narrow, depends on a new activity opportunity and declared scheduling, and
does not justify default adoption or a broad social-autonomy claim.017 remains
unprotected pending separate independent review. No018 was begun.

## Exact question and minimal architecture

Can individuals' actual activities, ongoing commitments, resources and independent
decisions change other individuals' interaction opportunities without laboratory
assignment of every availability change or social outcome?

The existing resource ECS already advances one event/tick and tracks busy participants.
An isolated017 adapter exposes that real commitment across subsequent choices. Two
people negotiate; the third sees only current availability and can choose meaningful
own consumption or Wait. Actual termination releases the pair. No future end/result
is supplied. Resource resolution, scarcity policy, consent/arbitration, old cognition,
Grouped assessment/FIFO retention and012 inquiry learning remain their established rules.
No new cognitive cells or calibrated willingness estimates are introduced.

Three people suffice for occupied-pair/free-person evidence, but every possible pair
overlaps. No simultaneous disjoint meetings are claimed. Inquiry stays atomic in every
mode; the third person receives no interior decision opportunities during a query.
This is a minimal resource-commitment study, not a uniform asynchronous scheduler.

## Endogenous versus externally established

| Class | Actual017 boundary |
|---|---|
| External initial conditions | Three people, inventories/traits/profiles, legitimate prior events/evidence/concerns, static public co-presence, fixed horizon, physical bounds, seed and order. |
| Actor choices | Invitations, independent incoming consent, old resource/inquiry actions, Wait, eligible own food consumption. |
| Endogenous consequences | Actual occupied pair and release, elapsed time, eaten/transferred food, hunger reduction, received evidence, real refusal/trust, concern and learned-expectation changes, later choices. |
| Observer-only | Full histories, hidden partner components, scenario label, future results and post-evaluation comparisons. |

No post-setup busy intervals, willingness/profile changes, hunger injections or food
refills occur. Hunger changes through established consumption only; this is not
endogenous metabolism. Meal opportunities and period boundaries remain laboratory
conditions. Mandatory old consumption still costs three ticks after each horizon.
Need often falls to zero and stocks can remain stranded with hungry nonowners.

## Controls and fixed local heuristic

Serial retains current-wave availability and atomic resource resolution; Committed
uses the same contact policy but steps real resource commitments. Neither offers
voluntary within-period consumption. SerialActive and Active respectively add the
same actor-local consumption choice. NoHistory is Active with only outgoing resource
ranking's own trust/episodes masked; actual histories, incoming responses, inquiry and
updates remain intact. It is not cognitive amnesia.

Every mode uses old History contact ranking except the declared NoHistory projection,
and the same Continue0 recipient control. Actual selected Invite grants outgoing
permission; selecting Consume/Wait does not. Consumption can finish in the send tick,
then its actor independently responds in the next tick. Old incoming valuations,
stable ties and provisional consent remain; acceptance can be displaced by an outgoing
accepted edge. No declined recipient is forced and no failed invitation trains006/012.

The pure activity selector ranks Wait0, eligible Consume at own hunger, then the
selected old Invite at its old score, using strict ties in that order. Food/hunger,
old local query and current public availability are the inputs, not future usefulness
or truth. Comparing these heterogeneous scores is a heuristic, not calibrated utility.
For example donation-oriented resource value98 beats hunger60 while partners are
available; actual occupation can remove that option and make consumption worth doing.

## Time and persistence

Send1/response1 are shared elapsed phases. Failed invitations cost2; resource admission
reserves8 including handshake, Direct15, Evidence33, while actual resolver time is
charged. Stepped resource scenes produce one actual event per tick. A free third
person's selected consumption shares that tick with the disjoint pair's event: two
actor activities do not become two serial world ticks. It uses one own food and
reduces hunger by40, with an explicit old-format record. Busy actors cannot consume,
initiate or respond elsewhere. No double booking, rollback, arbitrary busy extension
or invisible refund occurs. A resource Leave is an actual voluntary termination.

Current busy membership comes from the existing active-scene index, not a configured
deadline. Public input excludes scene outcomes, exact future release and private
partner state. Safe admission makes all commitments end by each period boundary;
cross-period commitments are unsupported. Tests cover budgets2/8/16/24/48 and actual
release. Same individuals carry food,hunger,trust,episodes,concerns,knowledge,retention,
inquiry estimates and histories across6–32 periods without reset.

## Ordering and initial-condition limits

For13 budget24 families under Active, Ascending/Descending/Rotating respectively
execute29/48/32 meetings,6/8/6 queries,17/32/22 transfers and21/21/21 consumption.
Under NoHistory transfers15/20/40; no universal ranking survives order. Stable partner
and incoming ID ties remain even when proposer arbitration rotates. Matching is legal,
not fair. Resource opening direction can make the same pair help or end in Leave.

Initial traits/co-presence and the selected consumption score are laboratory design,
not emergent social roles. Competing_eater intentionally permits own eating to compete
with contact; the effect's concentration there is a construction/generalization limit.
No policy sees the family label or future opportunity, and no desired pair is scripted.
The held-out horizon/period variants are coupled sensitivity, not isolated causal
estimates of time or evidence of natural social environments.
Old concern preparation runs at free-cohort observation boundaries, not every
interior resource tick. Changed later cohort-wave counts can therefore change concern
age and Rotating phase as well as resource access. Keep those mediators separate
from the first isolated consumption/availability slice; no general cadence optimum
has been identified.

## Scientific classification, verification and remaining question

Demonstrated: real selected resource commitments determine current availability;
independent consumption can change later incoming/outgoing opportunities, actual
transfers/relationships and persistent material state. The contact-only null is real.
Mechanism-dependent: three-person room, resource stepping versus atomic inquiry,
hunger/Invite score comparison, Continue0, conservative bounds, period closure and ID
order. Ambiguous: minimal opportunity cadence, isolated contribution of relationship
versus food history, ecological prevalence and whether different independent activities
would produce robust effects. Unsupported: calibrated motivation, fair allocation,
friendship,institutions,society,consciousness,sustainable economy or general intelligence.

No default adoption or017 foundation protection is warranted by this task. The
strongest remaining question is whether the narrow effect survives when several
worthwhile activities/partners are concurrently possible and needs recur through
explicit physical processes, rather than finite initial hunger and one free person.
